# AI Engineer Study Library

A free, unofficial study plan built from what two AI creators share in public: every resource mentioned in
[Bashiri Smith's Facebook reels](https://www.facebook.com/basweai), [Melvin Vivas's X posts](https://x.com/melvindvivas)
and the AI articles on [Melvin Vivas's blog](https://www.melvinvivas.com/), with a link back to each source.

**Open the library:** [ralmodiel.github.io/ai-study-library](https://ralmodiel.github.io/ai-study-library/)

[![15-second tour of the study library](docs/promo-preview.gif)](https://ralmodiel.github.io/ai-study-library/)

> **Unofficial.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bashiri Smith, BASWE.Ai or Melvin Vivas. The summaries and
> key points are AI-assisted (drafted with an AI model from the public posts, then checked by the maintainer) and
> may contain mistakes, so always check the original. Every entry links to it.

## What is inside

- **Free to watch:** the free videos, playlists and courses they recommend, grouped by topic in study order.
- **[Learning path](https://ralmodiel.github.io/ai-study-library/learning-path.html):** 16 topics in study order,
  from roadmaps and LLM fundamentals to RAG, agents, evals and deployment. Each has its own page with the reels and
  posts, the free resources, the tools and project ideas.
- **All resources:** every tool, course, book, repo and paper mentioned, with who recommended it and where.
- **Reels, posts and articles:** one note per item with a summary, key points and its links, filed by topic in
  [`docs/notes/`](docs/notes/) (`01-roadmap/` to `16-trends/`).
- **Guides:** the PDFs shared in captions, with every resource listed inside them.

The same content is in plain Markdown in [`docs/`](docs/): start with [docs/README.md](docs/README.md),
[the free watch list](docs/free-watch-list.md) or [the learning path](docs/learning-path.md).

> **About the content.** This repository holds no full copies of anyone's videos, audio, transcripts, posts,
> articles or PDFs: only AI-assisted summaries and key points in the library's own words, short excerpts such as
> commands or list items, and a link to every original. The originals belong to their creators.

## Share it

Pasting the site's link on Facebook, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Slack shows [this preview image](docs/og-image.png).
To post the tour as a video, use the 15-second [landscape cut](docs/promo.mp4) or the
[vertical reel](docs/promo-reel.mp4) and put the link in the caption. Please keep the "unofficial" wording when you
share it.
