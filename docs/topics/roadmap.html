Build

  • Build a real RAG system covering chunking, embeddings, a vector database, and semantic search. (from AI Engineer Roadmap Overview: From ML Foundations to RAG, Agents & Ops)
  • RAG app with hybrid search, reranking, and citations, evaluated on retrieval quality and answer accuracy. (from SWE-to-AI Engineer Plan for 2027: LLMs, RAG, Agents, Evals, Job Search)
  • Agent that researches companies or updates a CRM, with retries and a human approval step, evaluated on task success, latency, and cost. (from SWE-to-AI Engineer Plan for 2027: LLMs, RAG, Agents, Evals, Job Search)
  • A portfolio project chosen from an AI-assisted market gap analysis of what your target companies are hiring for. (from Step-by-Step Roadmap to a $200K+ AI Engineering Role)
  • Build a production-style LLM app that solves a real problem: LangGraph orchestration, LiteLLM model gateway, pgvector retrieval, Langfuse tracing, all containerized with Docker. (from 7 Habits to Become an AI Engineer: Books, Tooling, Research & Shipping)
  • Add a RAG pipeline with reranking and evals, deploy it to Vercel or AWS, and monitor it with OpenTelemetry, Grafana or LangSmith. (from 7 Habits to Become an AI Engineer: Books, Tooling, Research & Shipping)
  • Publish the project on GitHub with an architecture diagram and a technical write-up on LinkedIn. (from 7 Habits to Become an AI Engineer: Books, Tooling, Research & Shipping)
  • A checkpoint project: an end-to-end RAG app where you choose the models, build the retrieval pipeline (embeddings, vector database, chunking, hybrid retrieval), and explain why you made each choice. (from How to Relearn LLMs & RAG in 2026: A 7-Step Roadmap with Free Resources)
  • Build a RAG pipeline from scratch. (from 8-Week Roadmap to a $200K+ AI Engineering Role)
  • Build an AI agent system orchestrated with LangGraph or CrewAI. (from 8-Week Roadmap to a $200K+ AI Engineering Role)
  • Containerize an AI app with Docker, serve it with FastAPI, and add evals and monitoring. (from 8-Week Roadmap to a $200K+ AI Engineering Role)
  • A basic to-do app built without AI, as a check of your software fundamentals (from AI Engineer Roadmap Before 2027: Fundamentals, RAG, Agents, Ops, Evals)
  • A RAG pipeline built from scratch (from AI Engineer Roadmap Before 2027: Fundamentals, RAG, Agents, Ops, Evals)
  • Your own personal AI project after doing the practice projects (from AI Engineer Roadmap Before 2027: Fundamentals, RAG, Agents, Ops, Evals)
  • One production-grade LLM app with RAG for a focused use case using real data. It should include 30–50 evaluation test cases, hybrid search with reranking, a permissioned tool, regression tests for prompt injection and unauthorized access, tracing, and tracking of quality, latency and cost. Write it up as a public case study. (from 3-Month Roadmap to AI Engineering for Experienced Software Engineers)
  • A RAG app deployed with FastAPI and Docker, with evaluations, logging and failure handling, that tracks answer accuracy, latency and cost. (from AI Engineer Roadmap for 2026 in 60 Seconds)
  • An AI agent project with a test suite and failure handling. (from AI Engineer Roadmap for 2026 in 60 Seconds)
