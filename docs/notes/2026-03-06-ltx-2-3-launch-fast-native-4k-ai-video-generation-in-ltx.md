# LTX-2.3 Launch: Fast Native 4K AI Video Generation in LTX Studio

## Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares the launch announcement for LTX-2.3, a video generation model now powering LTX Studio. The promo video says the model generates native 4K video with built-in dialogue and audio, and claims it is the fastest 4K model available. The model is aimed at production workflows rather than one-off clips. The video teaches no technique; it is mainly a product announcement that's useful for keeping up with generative video tools.

## Key points

- LTX-2.3 is the newest LTX video model, described as 'the most production-ready model yet'.
- It generates native 4K video at industry-standard frame rates.
- It produces dialogue and audio natively, and the announcement says the audio is clearer than before.
- It supports portrait (vertical) video generation.
- Motion guidance uses a first frame and a last frame (keyframe conditioning).
- The announcement says LTX-2.3 is up to 5x faster than any other model, with sharper details and stronger motion. These are vendor claims, not independent benchmarks.
- LTX Studio presents itself as a full creative workflow 'from first frame to final delivery' that cuts down on revisions, not just a video generator.
- LTX-2.3 is available now in LTX Studio.
