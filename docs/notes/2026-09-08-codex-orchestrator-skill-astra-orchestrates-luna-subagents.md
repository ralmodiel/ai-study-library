## Summary

Melvin Vivas shares his open-source Codex skill. It sets up Astra (or Sol) as the orchestrator and hands subtasks to Luna subagents, a multi-agent pattern that saves cost.

## Key points

- Pattern: a strong orchestrator model plans and delegates; a cheaper model runs the subtasks as subagents.
- The skill supports Astra or Sol as the orchestrator and Luna as subagents in Codex.
- The skill is open source on GitHub.
