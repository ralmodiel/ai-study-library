# Open Models Aren't Always Local Models

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-14 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2088152238527328574)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The post separates 'open' (downloadable weights) from 'local' (small enough to run on your own computer). Many open-weight models are too big for consumer hardware, so be precise when you talk about them.

## Key points

- open != local: open weights don't mean the model fits on your machine.
- Whether a model runs locally depends on its size, quantization and your VRAM/RAM.
- Say which one you mean when recommending models to others.
