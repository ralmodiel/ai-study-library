## Summary

Meta released Muse Glimmer-30B as an open-weights model. Its published benchmarks beat Qwen3.6 37B on most general agentic tasks. Melvin plans to try it as the model behind the Hermes agent.

## Key points

- Muse Glimmer-30B is an open-weights model from Meta.
- According to the posted benchmarks, it beats Qwen3.6 37B on most general agentic tasks.
- He sees it as a good candidate model for local agent harnesses like Hermes.
