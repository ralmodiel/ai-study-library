# Cognition Releases SWE-1.7: Near-Frontier Coding Model at Low Cost

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-07-09 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2074905118861045986)

**Topics:** Industry Trends & Job Market, LLM Fundamentals, Fine-tuning & Model Customization · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

Shares Cognition's announcement of SWE-1.7, their most capable model so far. Cognition says it scores within a few points of the strongest frontier models at a fraction of the cost and runs at 1000 tokens/s. They also say that scaling RL with a refined recipe keeps producing gains.

## Key points

- SWE-1.7 is Cognition's most capable model to date.
- Scores within a few points of top frontier models at a fraction of the cost.
- Served at about 1000 tokens per second.
- Cognition says RL is 'not hitting its limit': after refining their recipe, gains continue as they scale.
