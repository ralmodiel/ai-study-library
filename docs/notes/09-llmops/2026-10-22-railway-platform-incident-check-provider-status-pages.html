Summary

Grok Bot told the creator that the Railway hosting platform was compromised, and he links Railway's official status page for the incident. It's a reminder to watch your deployment provider's status page during security incidents.

Key points

  • Railway (an app hosting/deployment platform) had an incident; its status page lists it as 'Platform Outage — Identified'.
  • The creator first heard about it from Grok Bot, then pointed to the official status page.
  • If you deploy on a third-party platform, follow its status page and check your secrets and credentials when there's a breach.
