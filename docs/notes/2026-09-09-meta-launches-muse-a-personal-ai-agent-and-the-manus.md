## Summary

Melvin Vivas reposts Meta's announcement of Muse, a personal AI agent that "gets things done across every part of life." He notes that it isn't available in Singapore yet and wonders how much of Manus AI's agent technology went into it. This is industry news about consumer AI agents, not a tutorial. The video has no speech.

## Key points

- Meta has announced Muse, a personal AI agent app that handles tasks across many parts of daily life.
- Muse is available as a downloadable app through muse.ai. When the link was checked, it went to an auth page and returned an error.
- The rollout is regional: the creator says Muse is not available in Singapore yet.
- The creator guesses that Manus AI's general-purpose agent technology may be part of Muse. This is his speculation, not a confirmed fact.
- The trend to watch: big tech companies are shipping consumer 'do-things-for-you' agents, not just chatbots.
