## Summary

The creator argues that software engineers who worry about job security should move into AI engineering. He says it is the most in-demand job in 2026, that about 80% of its skills are software engineering basics, and that the other 20% can be learned in about three months. He adds that it pays at least 20% more and makes it easier to move into new roles such as forward deployed engineer (FDE). The video ends with a pitch for his BASWE AI Engineer community.

## Key points

- AI engineering is presented as the most in-demand job in 2026 and the natural next step up from software engineering.
- About 80% of AI engineering skills are software engineering basics you already have.
- The remaining ~20% of AI-specific skills can be learned in about 3 months if you stay committed.
- Average salary and total compensation for AI engineers are said to be at least 20% higher than for software engineers.
- Being an AI engineer makes it easier to move into whatever role comes next as the field changes.
- Forward deployed engineer (FDE) roles are popular right now, but the creator says their requirements are basically those of an AI engineer.
