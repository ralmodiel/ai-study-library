## Summary

This is a short announcement that ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 video generation model can now be used through Replicate. The quoted post says Seedance 2.0 is open to all users. It takes text, images, video and audio together and turns them into one scene with synchronized stereo sound and realistic physics, in under 2 minutes.

## Key points

- Seedance 2.0 is a multimodal video generation model that is now open to all users.
- It takes text, images, video and audio as inputs and combines them into a single scene.
- Its output has synchronized stereo sound and realistic physics.
- According to the announcement, a generation finishes in under 2 minutes.
- You can also reach Seedance 2.0 through Replicate, a hosted model-API platform, which lets developers call it from code.
