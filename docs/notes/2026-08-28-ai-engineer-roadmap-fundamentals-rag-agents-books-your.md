# AI Engineer Roadmap: Fundamentals, RAG, Agents, Books & Your First AI Service

**Topics:** Start Here: Roadmaps & Strategy, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Bashiri Smith lays out a short roadmap for becoming an AI engineer before 2027. Start with how models work, using Karpathy's Zero to Hero and 3Blue1Brown's neural network series. Then learn the core of the job, RAG and agents, through LangChain's RAG From Scratch, LangGraph and the Hugging Face Agents Course, and back it up with two books. Finish by shipping a real AI service with FastAPI and Docker.

## Key points

- Learn fundamentals first. Tools change every few months, but engineers who understand what happens inside the model keep their edge.
- Step 1: Watch Andrej Karpathy's Neural Networks: Zero to Hero alongside 3Blue1Brown's Neural Networks series.
- Step 2: RAG and agents are the core of the job right now.
- For RAG, LangChain's RAG From Scratch series covers embeddings, vector databases, retrieval and how to run RAG in production.
- For agents, pick one orchestration framework and go deep. The creator picks LangGraph and pairs it with the Hugging Face Agents Course on multi-step, tool-using systems.
- Back this up with Chip Huyen's 'AI Engineering' and Michael Albada's 'Building Applications with AI Agents'.
- Take the production skills you already have and point them at AI. AI engineering is basically software engineering that ships AI.
- Step 3: Build an AI service with FastAPI, containerize it with Docker and put it behind a real API.
