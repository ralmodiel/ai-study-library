# Coworker: Free Local-First Desktop App for Running AI Agents

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-29 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2093548227039830065)

**Topics:** AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Melvin Vivas introduces Coworker, his free desktop app that runs AI agents on your own computer. It works with local models, free OpenRouter models and OpenAI. The agents can create files, research the web and run scheduled tasks with approvals, and you can reach them through Telegram. He lists the real tasks he has tried so far.

## Key points

- Coworker runs AI agents locally on your desktop (local-first).
- Model options: local models, free models on OpenRouter, and OpenAI.
- Agents do real tasks: create files, research the web, set up scheduled tasks.
- Supports scheduling and human approvals before actions run.
- Telegram integration lets you chat with your agent when you're away from your computer.
- Use cases he tried: create an invoice, research LinkedIn profiles for lead generation, schedule stock price updates, log a receipt from an image into a CSV.
