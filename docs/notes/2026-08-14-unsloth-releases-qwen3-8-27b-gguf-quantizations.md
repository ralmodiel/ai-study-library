# Unsloth releases Qwen3.8-27B GGUF quantizations

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-14 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2088282753313993016)

**Topics:** LLMOps, Deployment & Monitoring, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Announces that Unsloth has published GGUF builds of Qwen3.8-27B, so you can run it locally with llama.cpp-compatible tools. The post thanks Unsloth AI.

## Key points

- Unsloth publishes GGUF quantizations of new open models soon after they come out
- GGUF files work with llama.cpp-based local runtimes
