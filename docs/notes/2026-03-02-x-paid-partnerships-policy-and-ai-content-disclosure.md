## Summary

The creator points to X's paid partnerships policy, which goes with X's new option to label posts as paid partnerships or made with AI. He notes that Australia, the EU and the UK have their own country-specific policies that creators also need to read.

## Key points

- X now lets users label posts as paid partnerships or content made with AI.
- Tap the flag icon below the post box to add a disclosure.
- The paid partnerships policy is on help.x.com.
- Australia, the EU and the UK have separate country-specific policies to check.
