Run Notebooks on a Free GPU with Google Colab (T4, 15GB VRAM)

Summary

The post's caption is excited about Claude Opus 5.5 turning the creator's raw clips into shorts. The clip itself teaches one thing: you don't need to buy a GPU to start. Google Colab lets you log in with a Gmail account and run any notebook you find on a free T4 GPU with about 15GB of VRAM.

Key points

  • You don't need to buy a GPU to experiment with ML/AI notebooks.
  • Go to colab.research.google.com (or search 'Google Colab').
  • Log in with your Gmail (Google) account.
  • You can open and run any notebook you find, for example from GitHub or tutorials.
  • The free tier gives you an NVIDIA T4 GPU with about 15GB of VRAM.
  • Caption side note: the creator says Claude Opus 5.5 turned his raw video clips into short-form videos and jokes he may need a Claude Max plan.
