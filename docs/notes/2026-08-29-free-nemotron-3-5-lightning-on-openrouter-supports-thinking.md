## Summary

Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is available for free on OpenRouter and supports thinking (reasoning) mode. It also works in the creator's Coworker app.

## Key points

- Nemotron 3.5 Lightning is offered as a free model on OpenRouter.
- The model supports thinking (reasoning) mode.
- You can use it as the agent model inside Coworker.
