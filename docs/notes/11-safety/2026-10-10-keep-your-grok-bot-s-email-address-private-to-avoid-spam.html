Keep Your Grok Bot's Email Address Private to Avoid Spam

Summary

A short privacy tip. If you create your Grok Bot's email by tagging @bot publicly on X, don't put the address you want in the public post, or the bot's inbox will get spammed.

Key points

  • You create a Grok Bot email by tagging @bot on X.
  • Don't put the email address you want in a public post.
  • A publicly shared agent inbox invites spam, and possibly unwanted input to your agent.
