Summary

The creator says OpenAI's Astra is far ahead of Sol. In the quoted post, OpenAI says Astra is more token-efficient and better at finding vulnerabilities and developing exploits. OpenAI plans to release it with its most advanced cyber capabilities limited at first, then widen access over time.

Key points

  • Astra is described as more token-efficient and better at vulnerability discovery and exploit development.
  • OpenAI plans a staged rollout, limiting the most advanced cyber capabilities at first.
  • This is an example of gated release for dual-use security capabilities.
