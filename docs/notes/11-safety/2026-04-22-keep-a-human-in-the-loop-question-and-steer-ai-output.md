# Keep a human in the loop: question and steer AI output

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-04-22 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2046769129936597154)

**Topics:** AI Safety, Security & Guardrails, AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Advice to slow down and review AI output carefully instead of accepting it as-is. AI can be confidently wrong, so engineers need to understand, question and steer what it produces to stay in control.

## Key points

- Don't accept AI output without checking it.
- Understand it, question it, steer it.
- AI can be confidently wrong, and missing that means losing control.
- Human-in-the-loop review is essential, not optional.

## Try this

- [ ] Review and understand AI-generated output before using it.
- [ ] Question AI answers and steer the model when it is wrong.
