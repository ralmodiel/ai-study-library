Summary

Google's SynthID Detector is now open to everyone. It checks whether online content was made with Google AI or with tools from partners such as OpenAI, NVIDIA and Kakao, with Apple support coming soon. The creator notes that this lets you automate the question 'is it AI?'.

Key points

  • SynthID is Google's watermarking and detection technology for AI-generated content.
  • SynthID Detector is now available to everyone at synthid.com.
  • It detects content from Google AI and from industry partners: OpenAI, NVIDIA and Kakao, with Apple coming soon.
  • It can be used to automate checks on whether content was AI-generated.

Resources mentioned
