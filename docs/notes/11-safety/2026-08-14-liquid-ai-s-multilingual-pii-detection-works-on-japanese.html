Summary

A short X post with a 12-second video and no speech. Melvin Vivas shares a Liquid AI post that says its PII (personally identifiable information) handling also works on Japanese text. The lesson is that PII detection or redaction can work in languages other than English. The post gives no details on the model, setup or benchmarks.

Key points

  • PII detection and redaction can work in languages other than English. The quoted Liquid AI post says Japanese works too.
  • The video is a 12-second demo with no narration, so the clip itself has no technical details.
  • The source is a quoted post from Liquid AI (x.com/liquidai). The link in the caption is cut off, so open Liquid AI's X account to find the original post.
  • If your app handles personal data in several languages, check that your PII guardrail covers every language your users write in, not just English.
