# Apply While You Build: Get Job-Search Feedback on Your AI Project Early

## Summary

Bashiri Smith says the biggest mistake aspiring AI engineers make is waiting until a big portfolio project is finished before applying for jobs. Instead, he suggests adding the planned project to your resume, building it in 1–2 months, and applying at the same time. The responses you get, or don't get, show you whether the project and your resume match what employers want, before you've spent months on it.

## Key points

- Common mistake: spending 2–6 months on an 'impressive' project, then another 3–6 months applying, only to find that no one cares about it.
- Put the project on your resume early and start applying right away instead of waiting until it's done.
- Be honest about status: clearly label ongoing work, and separate intended (target) results from results you've actually measured.
- Aim to build the project in about 1–2 months while you apply.
- Expect roughly 1–2 months of applying before responses start coming in.
- If you get no responses, either the project or other parts of your resume are weak. Use applications and interviews to find gaps in your resume, positioning and skills.
- Adjust as you learn: don't polish a project for 6 months without checking that it shows what your target roles need.
- You don't need a perfect project to make progress in your job search.
