# Switching to Linux (Omarchy): Getting Through the First Two Weeks

Melvin Vivas · X video post · 2026-08-22 · 1:35 · 162 views · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2091153654858522854)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares a clip from DHH (creator of Ruby on Rails), who is seeing many people move from Mac and Windows to Omarchy, his Linux desktop setup. DHH says the first three days of his own switch felt like detox after 20 years of Mac muscle memory. The first week was still frustrating, but by the end of the second week he thought the new setup was great. The lesson is to push through the early pain. The clip doesn't cover AI engineering directly; it's about developer tooling and changing work habits.

## Key points

- Computing habits run deep. DHH had about 20 years of Mac muscle memory, expectations and taste for how things should look and work.
- Expect to press the wrong hotkeys, break things and dislike how things look or behave at first.
- Days 1-3 are the hardest. DHH calls them a 'detox' and says he wondered whether the switch would work at all.
- After about 3 days the worst frustration fades, though the rest of the first week is still a bit annoying.
- By the end of week 2 the new setup feels natural, and DHH went from 'what was the big deal?' to 'this is great.'
- DHH made his switch to Linux about 18 months before this clip.
- The quoted post says many people ("A LOT!!") are moving from Mac and Windows to Omarchy. His advice: stick with it.
