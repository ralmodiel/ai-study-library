## Summary

Melvin Vivas shares a demo of AuK, a new audio model from Tencent Hunyuan that combines text-to-speech and audio editing in one model. The caption says it handles zero-shot voice generation, speech editing, acoustic control, audio cleanup and source separation, and that it can remove an accent. The video is a run of before/after audio samples with no spoken explanation.

## Key points

- AuK is described as a unified audio generation and editing model from Tencent Hunyuan.
- Capabilities listed in the caption: zero-shot voice generation, speech editing, acoustic control, audio cleanup and source separation.
- The quoted post says AuK can edit out a speaker's accent.
- The demo plays paired samples (original, then edited) with the same sentence re-rendered, which shows content-preserving speech editing.
- Some samples show edited phrases (e.g. 'I'm not taking anything away from that' changed to 'I'm not taking any shots'), which shows text-based word replacement inside existing speech.
- The project page auk-project.github.io has the official demos and details.
