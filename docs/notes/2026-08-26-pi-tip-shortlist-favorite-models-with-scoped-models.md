**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

A productivity tip for the Pi coding agent, which connects to 15+ providers and hundreds of models. You can make a shortlist of favorite models with /scoped-models and switch between them with Ctrl+P.

## Key points

- Pi supports 15+ providers and hundreds of models.
- Use the /scoped-models command to pick a shortlist of favorite models.
- Press Ctrl+P to cycle through the shortlisted models.
