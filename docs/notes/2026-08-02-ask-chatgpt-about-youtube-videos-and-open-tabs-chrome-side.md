## Summary

This is a short demo of the ChatGPT Chrome extension's Side Chat, where you ask ChatGPT questions about a YouTube video. The quoted announcement says Side Chat also lets you refer to your open tabs or highlight text on a page and ask about it. It also says the ChatGPT desktop app now suggests URLs as you type. The transcript only has an intro line, so the caption is the only real content.

## Key points

- The ChatGPT Chrome extension has a Side Chat panel that opens next to the page you're viewing.
- In Side Chat you can ask questions about the YouTube video you're watching.
- You can refer to your other open tabs in Side Chat questions.
- You can highlight text on any web page and ask ChatGPT about it.
- The ChatGPT desktop app now suggests URLs as you type.
- Use case: get a summary or ask questions about long videos and articles without switching tabs.
