# Test models on your own workflow, not public benchmarks

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-29 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2104753013802078256)

**Topics:** Evaluation (Evals) & Testing, LLM Fundamentals · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Agreeing with a quoted post that is strongly against benchmarks, Melvin argues that the real test of a model is how it does on your own workflow and usage. Models can score well on public benchmarks and still do poorly in real-world use, so build your own evaluations.

## Key points

- Public benchmark scores don't reliably predict how a model performs on real tasks.
- The most meaningful benchmark is your own workflow and usage.
- A model can do great on public leaderboards and still be bad at real-world use.
- Before switching models, test candidates on your own representative tasks.

## Try this

- [ ] Test models on your own workflow and tasks instead of relying only on public benchmark scores.
- [ ] Build a small personal eval set from your real daily tasks and use it to compare models.
