Be Skeptical of Model Leaderboards: Muse Spark vs Astra

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 4 September 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Topics: Evaluation (Evals) & Testing, Industry Trends & Job Market · Level: beginner

Summary

The creator tried Muse Spark 1.3 in OpenCode and found it 'no way close to a GPT model'. He doubts an Artificial Analysis chart that ranks Muse Spark 1.3 Max above Astra, though he notes he tested the non-Max version. The lesson: check leaderboard rankings by testing models on your own tasks.

Key points

  • Hands-on, Muse Spark 1.3 felt much weaker than GPT models on coding.
  • The Artificial Analysis intelligence index reportedly ranked Muse Spark 1.3 Max above GPT-6 Astra, which the creator and the quoted poster doubt.
  • Model versions matter: the base model and the 'Max' version can score very differently, so compare the same versions.
  • Don't rely on one aggregate index. Test models yourself on real tasks.
