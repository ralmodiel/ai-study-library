## Summary

Melvin Vivas shows a short explainer video he made with OpenAI Codex and Hyperframes. He calls this workflow "cheating". The demo video explains "personal AI": an assistant that links a model to your own context, tools and rules. It covers five uses (memory, research, drafting, planning, automation) and ends with a short warning about access and privacy. The post doesn't explain how the video was built. It only shows the tool pairing and the result.

## Key points

- Workflow shown: a coding agent (OpenAI Codex) plus Hyperframes produces a short explainer video, about 50 seconds here. The post gives no build steps.
- Personal AI means a model connected to your context, your tools and your rules, so it doesn't forget you when the chat ends.
- Use 1, memory: it keeps your notes, decisions, preferences and projects so you don't have to re-explain yourself every week.
- Use 2, research: it searches your files, compares sources and returns the useful parts with citations ("receipts").
- Use 3, drafting: emails, outlines, summaries and code, with you acting as the editor.
- Use 4, planning: it turns goals and constraints into next actions that fit your calendar.
- Use 5, automation: rename, route, remind, update and report, so your own attention goes to decisions.
- Safety: context is power, so limit access, protect sensitive data and review important actions. It's a context-aware sidekick, not a digital clone.
