## Summary

Google released Veo 3.1 Lite, its most cost-efficient video generation model to date. Google also announced that the price of Veo 3.1 Fast would drop on April 7.

## Key points

- Veo 3.1 Lite is Google's lowest-cost video generation model so far.
- Veo 3.1 Fast was set to get a price cut on April 7.
- Comparing these tiers helps you balance cost against quality for video generation.
