# Opus 5.5 vs GPT-6 Sol for Writing: Latent Space AINews Test

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-23 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2102679214554243271)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, Evaluation (Evals) & Testing, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Melvin Vivas says Opus 5.5 is great at writing and quotes a Latent Space post. Latent Space ran its AINews newsletter generation side by side with GPT-6 Sol and Opus 5.5 and found Opus 5.5 far better. It made Opus 5.5 the default model for AINews.

## Key points

- Latent Space generated AINews side by side with GPT-6 Sol and with Opus 5.5.
- They describe the difference as 'night and day' in favor of Opus 5.5.
- Opus 5.5 is now the default model for AINews.
- Reasons given: more concise and tasteful reporting, and less 'slopese' (AI-sounding filler) than even Opus 5.
- Takeaway: compare candidate models on your own real task before choosing a default.
