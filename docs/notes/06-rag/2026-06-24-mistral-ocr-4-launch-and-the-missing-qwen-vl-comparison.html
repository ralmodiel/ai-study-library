Mistral OCR 4 launch, and the missing Qwen VL comparison

Topics: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Industry Trends & Job Market · Level: intermediate

Summary

Shares the launch of Mistral OCR 4, which outputs structured documents with bounding boxes, block classification and inline confidence scores across 170 languages. The creator points out that Mistral's announcement doesn't compare it with Qwen VL.

Key points

  • Mistral OCR 4 outputs bounding boxes, block classification and inline confidence scores.
  • It supports 170 languages.
  • Qwen VL is a vision-language alternative worth benchmarking against it for document parsing.

Resources mentioned

  • Mistral OCR 4 · tool · mistral.ai · paid
    Mistral's OCR model that produces structured document output with bounding boxes, block types and confidence scores.
  • Qwen VL · tool · github.com · free
    Alibaba's Qwen family of vision-language models, which can be used for OCR and document understanding.
