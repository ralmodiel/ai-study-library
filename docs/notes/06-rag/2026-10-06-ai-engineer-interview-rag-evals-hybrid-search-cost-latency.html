Summary

This is a mock AI engineer interview that answers three common questions in short form. It covers how to evaluate a production RAG pipeline end to end (retrieval metrics plus answer quality, latency and cost), when to use keyword search, vector search or both with a re-ranker, and how to cut cost and latency at a million queries a day without losing quality. It ends by promoting the creator's interview-questions video and his Skool community.

Key points

  • Evaluate RAG retrieval and the final answer separately.
  • Recall@K: how much of the relevant information was retrieved. Precision@K: how much of what was retrieved is actually relevant.
  • Answer-level checks: is the answer supported by evidence (grounded), is it complete, and are its sources cited correctly.
  • Also track operational metrics: response time and cost per query.
  • Keyword search is best for exact matches (e.g. error codes). Vector search finds similar meaning even when the wording differs. Combine them (hybrid) when you need both.
  • A re-ranker takes the first search results and checks which passages actually answer the question best.
  • Cost/latency at scale: first profile what is slow and expensive, then cache results that are safe to reuse, remove unneeded model calls, and send simpler questions to a smaller model.
  • Protect quality by testing every optimization on the same fixed question set before rollout (regression testing).
