5 RAG Metrics: Top-k, Recall@k, Precision@k, Top-p and Cost per Request

Summary

A short explainer of five numbers every AI engineer working on RAG should understand. It covers retrieval settings and metrics (top-k, recall@k, precision@k) with worked percentage examples. It also covers the generation sampling setting top-p and the cost of answering one question. The creator offers a RAG cheat sheet and a link to his AI engineer community.

Key points

  • Top-k sets how many chunks the retriever returns. With k=5 you get the 5 highest-scoring chunks.
  • Recall@k = relevant chunks retrieved in the top k / all relevant chunks. Example: 2 of 4 relevant chunks found = 50% recall.
  • Precision@k = relevant chunks in the top k / k. Example: 2 relevant out of 5 returned = 40% precision.
  • Raising k usually helps recall but can hurt precision. Tune k with both metrics in view.
  • Top-p (nucleus sampling) controls generation, not retrieval. At 0.9 the model keeps the most likely next tokens until their combined probability reaches 90%, then samples from that set.
  • Cost per request = retrieval + any reranking + all model calls needed to answer one question. Track it as a production metric.
