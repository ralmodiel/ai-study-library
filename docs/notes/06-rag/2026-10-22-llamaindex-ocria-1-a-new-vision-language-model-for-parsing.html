LlamaIndex OCRia-1: A New Vision-Language Model for Parsing Document Tables

Summary

Melvin Vivas shares LlamaIndex's launch of OCRia-1, a vision-language model (VLM) for document parsing and OCR. It is built mainly for complex tables like balance sheets, insurance schedules and financial filings. LlamaIndex says it sets new state-of-the-art results for accuracy, cost and latency on its own ParseBench and on third-party benchmarks such as olmOCR-bench. It is available now in LlamaParse.

Key points

  • OCRia-1 is a frontier VLM from LlamaIndex built for document parsing. Its main focus is complex tables.
  • Claimed scores: 87.4 on ParseBench (a new high), and 83.5 overall on olmOCR-bench, with 92.9 on tables.
  • LlamaIndex says it leads three third-party benchmarks as well as its own, and improves on accuracy, cost and latency (price-performance).
  • Training approach: trained on the hardest tables LlamaIndex could find, and graded on table structure and meaning, not just text accuracy.
  • Target uses: business documents where key data lives in tables, such as balance sheets, insurance schedules and financial filings.
  • It is live in LlamaParse, which LlamaIndex says has parsed billions of pages.
  • Launch offer: up to $3,000 in credits for enterprises for 7 days.

Try this

  • Try OCRia-1 in LlamaParse on your hardest table-heavy documents, like the creator plans to.
  • When comparing OCR or parsing models, check benchmarks like ParseBench and olmOCR-bench, and look at table scores separately.
  • Build a RAG pipeline over financial filings or insurance schedules that uses LlamaParse with OCRia-1 to extract tables, and compare answer quality against a basic text-only parser.
