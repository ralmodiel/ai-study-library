The 5 Levels of RAG: From Naive Retrieval to Agentic RAG

Summary

Bashiri Smith describes five levels of Retrieval-Augmented Generation, counting down from level 5 to level 1. They go from naive embed-and-retrieve pipelines to smarter chunking, hybrid search with reranking, advanced RAG with query rewriting and citation checks, and finally agentic RAG. For a first pipeline he points to ChromaDB and the LangChain Quickstart, and for chunking he points to LangChain's text splitters.

Key points

  • Level 5 – Naive RAG: embed your documents into a vector store, retrieve the top-k chunks and put them into the prompt. ChromaDB plus the LangChain Quickstart gets a first pipeline working.
  • Level 4 – Smarter chunking: replace blind fixed-size splits with recursive splitting, section-aware chunks and metadata so retrieval finds the right context. LangChain's text splitters cover this.
  • Level 3 – Reranking and hybrid search: combine dense vector search with BM25 keyword search and merge the results with Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF).
  • Level 2 – Advanced RAG: query rewriting, multi-hop retrieval and citation verification, so every claim is grounded in a source and checked.
  • Level 1 – Agentic RAG: agents decide what to retrieve and when, retry when results are poor, and correct themselves at scale.
