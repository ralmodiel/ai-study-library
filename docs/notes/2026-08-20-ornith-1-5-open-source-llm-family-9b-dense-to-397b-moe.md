# Ornith-1.5: Open-Source LLM Family (9B Dense to 397B MoE)

## Summary

Ornith-1.5 is a new family of open-source LLMs in three sizes: 9B dense, 35B MoE and 397B MoE. The models were trained with self-improving strategies. Its authors say it is state-of-the-art among open models of similar size and close to Claude Opus. The creator calls it a new model to try.

## Key points

- Three sizes: 9B dense, 35B Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) and 397B MoE.
- Trained with self-improving strategies.
- The authors claim state-of-the-art results among open-source models of comparable size.
- The creator says it is comparable to Opus 4.8. Check this against independent benchmarks.
