## Summary

Points to Fireworks as a provider for GLM-5.2. The quoted post says Artificial Analysis ranks GLM-5.2 #3 overall on GDPval-AA (1524 Elo) and #1 among open-weights models, ahead of the next by 116 points. It is useful for choosing an open-weights model and a place to host it.

## Key points

- GLM-5.2 is available on Fireworks AI.
- Artificial Analysis ranks it #3 overall on the GDPval-AA benchmark with 1524 Elo.
- It is #1 among open-weights models, ahead of the next one by 116 points.
- It was the most popular new model on Fireworks during the week of the post.
