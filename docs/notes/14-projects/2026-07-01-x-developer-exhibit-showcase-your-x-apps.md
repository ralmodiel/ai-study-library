## Summary

X announced the Developer Exhibit, a place where builders can list their X apps to get traffic and revenue. You submit and verify your app from the apps page of the X developer console. Melvin recommends building on X.

## Key points

- The Developer Exhibit is a showcase where builders can list apps built on the X platform.
- To list an app, submit and verify it from the apps page of the X developer console.
- Listings are meant to bring builders traffic and revenue.
