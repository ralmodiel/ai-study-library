Summary

The video shows a quick way to find portfolio project ideas that show real-world skill. You go to a research-paper listing site (not named on screen, but the categories match arXiv), open a field like AI or machine learning, and look at the newest entries. Then you screenshot 5–10 of them, paste the screenshots into ChatGPT with a prompt, and get 5–10 project ideas with steps and reasons for each. The creator says this approach can "condense two years of work experience into one coding project."

Key points

  • Goal: build one strong project based on current research, so it shows the kind of experience employers want.
  • Open the research listing website shown in the video. The category names match arXiv subject listings.
  • Pick the topic that interests you most. Suggested: Artificial Intelligence; Computational Engineering, Finance, and Science; or Machine Learning.
  • Click "see today's new changes" so the list shows only the newest entries.
  • Take about 5–10 screenshots that cover as many of the new entries as possible.
  • Paste the screenshots into ChatGPT along with the creator's prompt. The prompt isn't shown in the transcript; it comes with the guide you get by commenting.
  • ChatGPT should return 5–10 project ideas, each with exact steps and a reason it's worth doing.

Try this

  • Go to the research listing site (arXiv) and pick a category: Artificial Intelligence; Computational Engineering, Finance, and Science; or Machine Learning.
  • Click "see today's new changes" to see only the latest entries.
  • Take 5–10 screenshots covering as many new entries as you can.
  • Paste the screenshots into ChatGPT with the project-idea prompt and review the 5–10 ideas it gives you.
  • Comment "PROJECTS" to get the creator's step-by-step guide and the exact prompt.
  • Build a coding project based on a recent arXiv paper in AI, ML, or Computational Engineering/Finance/Science, using ChatGPT's step-by-step plan.
