Summary

This post shows a quick way to come up with portfolio projects for AI engineering jobs. You screenshot the Tasks list on Hugging Face's Models page and paste it into Claude with a detailed prompt. The prompt asks for a project a mid-level software engineer moving into AI engineering could build alone in 1–3 months, showing production-grade RAG, agents, evals or LLMOps. Most of the post promotes the creator's paid BASWE.AI Engineer community.

Key points

  • Go to huggingface.co, click 'Models', then find the 'Tasks' section.
  • Screenshot the whole list of tasks and paste it into Claude.
  • The prompt's limits: the project should stand out in the 2026 job market, suit a mid-level SWE moving into AI, use one or more Hugging Face tasks, show production-grade RAG, agents, evals or LLMOps, take one engineer 1–3 months, use real-world data and solve a practical problem.
  • The prompt also asks for careful system design, evaluation and scalability.
  • Ask for: project name, what it does, the problem it solves, the Hugging Face tasks used, a recommended tech stack, how it maps to real AI engineering job descriptions, and impact metrics ready for a résumé.
  • The creator says getting the idea is the easy part. Building it to production grade and presenting it on your résumé matter more.
