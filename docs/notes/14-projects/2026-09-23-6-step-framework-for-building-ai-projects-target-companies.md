# 6-Step Framework for Building AI Projects Target Companies Care About

## Summary

This short reel (no speech, caption only) gives a six-step process for picking and building a portfolio project. The project should solve a problem your target companies are already hiring people to solve. The steps are: choose target companies, research their real problems, pick one problem and go deep, design the system, build it, then launch and promote it. It ends with a pitch for the creator's BASWE.AI program.

## Key points

- Core rule: your next project should solve a problem your target companies are already hiring people to solve.
- Step 1 – Choose target companies: decide on industry, role, and company stage, and list 10–100 companies that fit your experience and goals.
- Step 2 – Research their real problems: read job descriptions, engineering blogs, company news, and tech stacks. Look for problems that keep coming up, and connect with engineers on those teams before you apply.
- Step 3 – Pick one problem and go deep: it should be specific, relevant to several target companies, and buildable in 1–2 months. Research the domain and ask engineers informed questions.
- Step 4 – Design the system: map out the architecture and decide whether AI actually helps. Choose models and tools, plan for reliability, cost, and evaluation, and be ready to explain each choice.
- Step 5 – Build it, alone or with a team, and document the decisions you make along the way.
- Step 6 – Launch it and get it seen: publish a clean repo, a clear README, a demo, and a writeup of the problem and your approach. Share it on LinkedIn and in relevant communities, and use it in applications and outreach.
