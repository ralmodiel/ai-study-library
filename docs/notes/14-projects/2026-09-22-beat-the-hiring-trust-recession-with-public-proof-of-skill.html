Beat the Hiring "Trust Recession" With Public Proof-of-Skill Projects

Summary

The creator says candidates aren't getting interviews because companies no longer trust résumés and credentials. He cites a 2026 AI hiring report (he doesn't name the platform) that describes a "trust recession." His fix is to pick a real problem companies care about, build a solution in public, and keep improving and posting about it like a mini startup until you get hired. That gives hiring managers proof they can actually evaluate.

Key points

  • The cited 2026 AI hiring report (platform not named) surveyed 400+ hiring managers, 200+ recruiters and 1,000+ job seekers.
  • Key finding: hiring is in a 'trust recession.' Over 70% of hiring managers worry about fake credentials.
  • Over 90% of hiring managers say they've met candidates who lie about their skills.
  • The way to earn trust is undeniable public proof of your skills through public projects.
  • Choose a problem companies care about and that you're uniquely positioned to solve.
  • Keep working on the problem and posting about it until you land a job. Don't build once and stop.
  • Treat the project like a mini startup: share your improvements publicly so hiring managers have something concrete to evaluate.
