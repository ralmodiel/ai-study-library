# Qwen3.5 0.8B Real-Time Video Captioning on Mac Studio

Melvin Vivas · X video post · 2026-03-26 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2037050598735176057)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

This reshares the same demo: Qwen3.5 0.8B (about 1GB) captions video locally on a Mac Studio M2 Ultra at under 1s per frame. It streams scene descriptions while the video plays.

## Key points

- The roughly 1GB Qwen3.5 0.8B model handles real-time video captioning.
- Under 1s per frame; 269 frames from a 3m49s video.
- Runs fully locally on a Mac Studio M2 Ultra.
- It understands the scene at any point where you pause.
