# Teaching Teens AI Safety: ChatGPT for Teens in Singapore

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-26 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2092594969408393322)

**Topics:** AI Safety, Security & Guardrails · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator, who sits on a parent support group committee at a Singapore secondary school, asks for an OpenAI contact in Singapore to run a student talk on AI safety and awareness. He says teens need to learn to use AI safely, responsibly and critically, not just how to use it, and links OpenAI's ChatGPT for Teens announcement.

## Key points

- OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens, built for learning and backed by protections.
- AI education for teens should cover safe, responsible and critical use, not just how to use the tools.
- Schools and parent groups can bring in AI providers for safety workshops.
