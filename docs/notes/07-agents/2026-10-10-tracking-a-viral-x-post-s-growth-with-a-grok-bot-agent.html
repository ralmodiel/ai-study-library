Tracking a Viral X Post's Growth with a Grok Bot Agent

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 10 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

Melvin Vivas used a Grok Bot agent to track a viral post. It checked the views on a schedule, judged whether growth was speeding up or slowing down, logged the data to Google Sheets and wrote a report with graphs at the end. It's a concrete example of a monitoring agent that runs on a schedule.

Key points

  • The bot checked the post's views every 30 minutes.
  • It reported whether growth was speeding up or slowing down.
  • It logged every data point to a Google Sheet.
  • At the end it wrote a report with 6 graphs.
  • Result: 2.32M views, with a peak of 176K views per hour.
  • The whole workflow was set up just by asking Grok Bot in plain language.
