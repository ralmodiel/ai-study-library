# Morning digest from a chief-of-staff agent

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-10-02 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2105858275996741808)

**Topics:** AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

The creator's primary 'chief of staff' Grok Bot sends him a morning digest. It finds leads, saves them to a Google Sheet, and includes updates from his other bots. This is a practical example of a personal assistant agent that orchestrates other bots.

## Key points

- A primary orchestrator bot sends a daily morning digest.
- The digest includes status updates gathered from the other bots.
- The bot finds leads and saves them to a Google Sheet.
- The orchestrator bot acts as a single daily touchpoint for a multi-bot setup.
