# Logging blood pressure from a screenshot with a personal AI agent

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-12 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2087457623125963196)

**Topics:** AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, Portfolio Projects · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator sends a screenshot of his blood pressure monitor to his Hermes AI assistant. Hermes reads the three readings and adds them to a CSV he can show his doctor. This is a simple, practical use of vision plus tool use to automate a daily chore.

## Key points

- Take a photo or screenshot of the device display and send it to the agent.
- The agent reads the 3 data points from the image.
- The agent appends a record to a CSV file.
- The CSV doubles as a health log to share with a doctor.
- This replaces typing the numbers into a notes app by hand every day.
