Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares the announcement of Devin Oncall, a product that uses the Devin AI agent to investigate alerts and bugs on its own and help teams diagnose and fix production incidents. The announcement says it already handles thousands of alerts a day for some of the world's largest companies and pitches it as an AI SRE. It is a short news item about AI agents moving into production support and on-call work.

Key points

  • Devin Oncall investigates production alerts and bugs automatically.
  • Its job is to help engineering teams diagnose and resolve production incidents, the work an SRE or on-call engineer usually does.
  • The announcement says it already handles thousands of alerts every day for some of the world's largest companies.
  • The creator's point is that production support engineers lose less sleep when an AI agent takes the first look at alerts.
  • Trend signal: autonomous coding agents are spreading beyond writing code into operations and incident response.
