Summary

The creator updated his Codex skill, in which a strong model (Astra or Sol) orchestrates and cheaper Luna subagents do the work. The update enforces Luna more strictly for tasks like research and codebase exploration.

Key points

  • Pattern: a strong model orchestrates and cheaper models run the subagents.
  • Send research and exploration tasks to the cheaper Luna subagents.
  • The update makes Codex use Luna more reliably for those tasks.
  • Astra or Sol can be the orchestrator.
