Summary

The Grok Bot marketplace is open for plugin (connector) submissions. Developers can submit connectors so Grok Bot works with their app.

Key points

  • The Grok Bot marketplace now accepts third-party plugins and connectors.
  • Submitting a connector lets Grok Bot work directly with your app.
  • The submission link in the post was cut off (x.ai/bot/marketplace/submit-a-plugin) and looked broken when checked.
