Summary

A one-off speed comparison on a simple task: turning text into a PDF. Hermes running Grok 4.6 took about 1 minute and ChatGPT Work took 3m 34s, so Hermes was more than 3× faster. This is a single anecdote, not a benchmark.

Key points

  • Task: convert text to a PDF
  • Hermes with Grok 4.6: about 1 minute
  • ChatGPT Work: 3m 34s
