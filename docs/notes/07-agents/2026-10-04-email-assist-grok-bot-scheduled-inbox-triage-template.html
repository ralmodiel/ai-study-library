Summary

Melvin shares a Grok Bot template called "Email Assist". It checks his inbox on weekday mornings and only notifies him when an email actually matters. It's a ready-to-copy example of a scheduled agent that filters email.

Key points

  • The bot runs on a schedule: weekday mornings.
  • It reads the inbox and filters for important messages.
  • It only pings you when something matters, which cuts down on notification noise.
  • The template is shared publicly on x.ai so you can copy and adapt it.
