Auto-research sales leads by forwarding email to an AI agent

Summary

The creator gives Grok Bot its own email inbox and sets up a Gmail filter that forwards incoming leads to it. The bot then researches each potential client and their business on its own. It shows a simple way to trigger an agent from email.

Key points

  • Grok Bot has its own inbox and can act on emails sent to it
  • Set up a Gmail filter to auto-forward lead emails to the bot's address
  • The bot researches the potential client and summarizes their business
  • Email forwarding is an easy, no-code way to trigger an agent workflow
