Summary

The creator shares his first Grok Bot template, 'Steve'. Every morning it scans what changed in AI and sends 3 ranked business bets, each with who pays, the offer, why now and a first move, plus one task to do today. You can customize the template for your own interests after installing it.

Key points

  • It runs every morning as a scheduled agent that scans recent changes in AI and the markets.
  • It sends 3 ranked product or service bets you could sell or ship.
  • Each bet covers: who pays, the offer, why now, and your first move.
  • It adds one 'do this today' action.
  • You can customize the topics after installing (the creator focuses on AI ideas).
  • It is aimed at founders and AI engineers looking for near-term income.
