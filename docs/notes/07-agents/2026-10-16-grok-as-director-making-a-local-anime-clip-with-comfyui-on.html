Summary

Melvin Vivas shows a 40-second anime episode he made on his own machine. Grok Bot acted as the "director" that coordinated production, ComfyUI was the generation "studio," and an RTX 3090 GPU ran everything. Images came from the Krea-2 Turbo model in FP8. The post is a short demo, not a step-by-step tutorial: the transcript is only character dialogue, and the caption is cut off before the rest of the model list.

Key points

  • Split roles: an LLM bot (Grok) acts as director and coordinates production, and ComfyUI does the image/video generation.
  • Everything ran locally on one consumer GPU, an NVIDIA RTX 3090 (24 GB VRAM).
  • Images were made with Krea-2 Turbo in FP8. FP8 cuts VRAM use so larger models fit on consumer cards.
  • The result was a 40-second anime episode with character dialogue.
  • The caption is truncated, so the full model list (likely video and voice models) isn't shown. Check the original thread for details.
