Chain Grok Bots to Get Trending X Posts

Summary

The creator describes a two-bot setup. An X researcher bot finds trending posts, and a 'chief of staff' Grok bot named Emma sends them to him, so he stays current with little effort.

Key points

  • One bot, the X researcher, collects trending posts.
  • A second bot, the chief-of-staff assistant 'Emma', sends him the findings.
  • Splitting roles across bots (researcher and assistant) is a simple multi-agent pattern.
  • He uses the setup as a way to keep up with trends.
