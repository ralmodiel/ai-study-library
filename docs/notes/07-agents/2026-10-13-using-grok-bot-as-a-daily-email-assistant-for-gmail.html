Summary

The creator linked his main Gmail accounts to Grok Bot so an 'Email Assist' bot checks them every day. The bot reports only the important emails, so he no longer has to check his inbox by hand. It's a simple example of a personal assistant agent connected to a tool.

Key points

  • Connect your Gmail accounts to Grok Bot.
  • Set up an 'Email Assist' bot that runs a check every day.
  • The bot reports only the important emails you shouldn't miss.
  • This replaces checking your inboxes by hand.
