A 'Chief of Staff' Primary Bot to Manage Multiple Grok Bots

Summary

The creator uses Grok Bot's new Primary Bot feature to make a 'Chief of Staff' bot. It summarizes what is happening across all his specialized bots, so he doesn't have to check each one. He can also ask it to delegate work to the other bots and report back, which is a simple orchestrator pattern for multiple agents.

Key points

  • Grok Bot's new Primary Bot feature lets one bot oversee your other bots.
  • He made a 'Chief of Staff' bot that reports what is happening across all his bots.
  • This saves checking each specialized bot one by one.
  • The primary bot can delegate work to other bots and report back, like an orchestrator agent over worker agents.
