Summary

The creator plans to test his Astra orchestrator + Luna subagents setup more thoroughly. He also wants to try other model mixes, such as adding Terra, to make Codex more efficient. The Plus plan's 5-hour usage limits are slowing his testing.

Key points

  • Goal: make coding with Codex more efficient by mixing models across orchestrator and subagent roles.

  • The Plus plan's 5-hour usage limits make heavy multi-agent testing hard.
