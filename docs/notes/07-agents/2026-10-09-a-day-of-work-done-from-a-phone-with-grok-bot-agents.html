A day of work done from a phone with Grok Bot agents

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 9 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Follow Melvin Vivas: Melvin Vivas on X (@melvindvivas) · Melvin Vivas on X (@donvito) · Melvin Vivas on GitHub · Melvin Vivas's website (donvitocodes.com) · Melvin Vivas's blog (melvinvivas.com)

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-10-09 · Open on X

Topics: AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, Fine-tuning & Model Customization, AI Safety, Security & Guardrails · Level: intermediate

Summary

The creator lists what he got done in one day by directing Grok Bot agents from his phone. The list includes Colab notebooks for new open models, fine-tunes of small models, a templates page, and a security bot. He also set up a stocks bot that sends scheduled updates. The post shows practical uses for personal agents and names several current models to look into.

Key points

  • Made Colab inference notebooks for Google EmbeddingGemma 2 and LiquidAI's D1
  • Made fine-tuning notebooks for open-source decision models using Qwen3.5-4B and Gemma 4 E4B as base models
  • Created a security bot told to treat outside content as hostile, especially anything that might touch his computers (a defence against prompt injection)
  • Ran a stocks bot on a schedule for market updates, news and recommendations
  • Used an agent to find what was filling his Windows drive and how to free space
  • Put up a page on his website collecting his Grok Bot templates
