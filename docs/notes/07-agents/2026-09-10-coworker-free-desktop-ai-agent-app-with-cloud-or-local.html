Coworker: Free Desktop AI Agent App with Cloud or Local Models

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 10 September 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

Melvin Vivas shows Coworker, his open-source desktop app where AI agents do work for you, such as making an invoice. In the demo it runs on the free Nemotron-3.5 Lightning model through OpenRouter. It also works with local models through Ollama or LM Studio, so your data stays on your computer.

Key points

  • Coworker is a free, open-source desktop app where AI agents do tasks for you, such as making invoices.
  • The demo uses the free Nemotron-3.5 Lightning model through OpenRouter.
  • It supports local models through Ollama and LM Studio if you don't want data to leave your machine.
  • The same agent app can switch between cloud and local model providers.
