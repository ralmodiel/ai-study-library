Grok Bot 0.70.2: Bot Email, Live App Cards and X Search

Summary

Release notes for Grok Bot 0.70.2, an agent product. Each bot can now get its own email address and draft emails. Apps like Figma, Notion and Canva appear as live cards in the chat, and bots can search public X posts and profiles without connecting an account.

Key points

  • Grok Bot 0.70.2 is rolling out
  • Each bot can claim its own email address at @mail.grokbot.com and draft emails for you to send
  • Figma, Notion and Canva show up as live interactive cards right in the chat
  • Bots can search X and read public posts and profiles without you connecting an account
  • These are examples of agent tool use: email, third-party app integrations and web/social search
