A 'Chief of Staff' Bot as Orchestrator for Other Bots

Summary

The creator explains that his 'COS' (chief of staff) bot started as an orchestrator. He told it to oversee his other bots and had them report to it, and the setup grew from there instead of starting from a template. The lesson is a simple multi-agent pattern: one supervisor agent coordinating several worker agents.

Key points

  • COS = a 'chief of staff' agent that sits above the other bots
  • Its first instruction was to oversee the other bots and have them report back to it
  • This is the orchestrator (supervisor) multi-agent pattern
  • No ready-made template: the setup was refined bit by bit over time
  • Start with a simple supervisor role and add duties as needs show up
