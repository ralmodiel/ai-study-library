Summary

The creator lists the plugins they connect to Grok Bot: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Stripe, Canva, Google Slides and Notion. They plan to turn these setups into templates. The list shows which tool integrations are most useful for productivity agents.

Key points

  • Plugins in use: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Stripe, Canva, Google Slides, Notion.
  • Connecting email, calendar, documents, payments and design tools lets one bot handle complete workflows.
  • The creator plans to publish templates based on these plugin combinations.
