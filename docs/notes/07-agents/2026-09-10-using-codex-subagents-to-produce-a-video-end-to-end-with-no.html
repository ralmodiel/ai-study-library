Summary

Melvin Vivas shows an 18-second promo-style video that he says OpenAI Codex made with subagents and no manual editing. According to the caption, separate subagents found and downloaded b-roll and music, produced the voiceover and motion graphics, and reviewed the result. The post is a short demo of splitting a creative pipeline across multiple agents. It does not explain how the setup works.

Key points

  • Codex can coordinate subagents, each handling one part of a larger multi-step task such as making a video.
  • The pipeline in the caption covers sourcing b-roll, sourcing music, producing the voiceover, adding motion graphics, and a final review pass.
  • The creator says the finished video needed no manual edits.
  • Giving one agent the review step acts as a built-in quality check before output.
  • The sample output is a short ad for an AI assistant called 'Coworker' ('remembers what matters, brings the right skills and keeps you in control').
  • The post gives no prompts, configuration, or tool details. It's a capability demo, not a tutorial.
