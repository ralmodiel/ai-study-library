## Summary

News that Grok Bot can now connect to go-to-market tools such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Clay and Granola. Sales teams can use it to track accounts, finish follow-ups and do deep research. It is an example of an agent using business tools through integrations.

## Key points

- Grok Bot can connect to Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Clay, Granola and other GTM tools.
- Use cases: staying on top of accounts, completing follow-ups and doing deep research.
- It is an example of agents becoming useful for business teams through integrations with existing tools.
