Summary

A quoted thread lists things worth building with Pi Durable and Cloudflare Durable Objects. The first is durable agents for high-stakes workflows. Pi Durable can detect interrupted operations and avoid automatically replaying unsafe calls, such as an agent that crashes partway through refunding a customer.

Key points

  • Pi Durable combined with Cloudflare Durable Objects gives you durable, stateful agents.
  • It suits high-stakes workflows where a crash partway through must not cause duplicate side effects.
  • Pi Durable identifies interrupted operations after a crash.
  • It avoids automatically replaying unsafe tool calls, such as issuing a refund twice.
