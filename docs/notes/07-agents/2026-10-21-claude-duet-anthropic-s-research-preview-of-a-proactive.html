Claude Duet: Anthropic's Research Preview of a Proactive Agent App

Summary

Melvin Vivas shares Anthropic's announcement of Claude Duet, a research-preview app where Claude works as a proactive agent. It tells you what needs your attention, completes tasks you give it, keeps working when you step away, and asks before acting for you. In the clip, a speaker credits Opus 5.5 with the judgment to know when to step in and what information matters, such as pulling relevant Slack updates. Vivas frames Duet as Anthropic's answer to Grok Bot and Dots.

Key points

  • Claude Duet is a new Anthropic app, released as a research preview, where Claude acts as a proactive agent instead of only answering when asked.
  • Duet has four main behaviors: it tells you what needs your attention, completes tasks you ask for, keeps working when you step away, and checks with you before acting on your behalf.
  • The checking step is a human-in-the-loop design: the agent works on its own but asks for approval before taking actions for you.
  • The speaker says Opus 5.5 gives the agent the judgment to decide when to be proactive and how to act for the user.
  • Context awareness matters: the agent separates information you need from information you don't.
  • Example use: it pulls the Slack messages and updates you need and brings them to you without being asked.
  • The speaker says Duet is now the main way they use Claude on their phone.
  • The post presents Duet as Anthropic's answer to competing proactive-agent products named Grok Bot and Dots.
