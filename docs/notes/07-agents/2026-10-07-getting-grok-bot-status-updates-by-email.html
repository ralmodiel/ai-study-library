Summary

Once his Grok Bot claimed its own inbox, Melvin had it send him updates by email. This turns email into a notification channel for the bot's work.

Key points

  • Ask your Grok Bot to send an email, and it claims an inbox at name@mail.grokbot.com.
  • The bot can send and receive email.
  • Have the bot email you status updates on its tasks.
