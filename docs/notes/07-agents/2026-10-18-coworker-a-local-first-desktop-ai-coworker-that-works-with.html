Coworker: a local-first desktop AI coworker that works with any model

Summary

The creator asks whether he should keep building Coworker, his local-first desktop AI coworker app. He says Grok Bot has already solved the personal/work AI assistant. Coworker could be a 'poor man's Grok Bot' because it works with any model.

Key points

  • Coworker is the creator's local-first desktop app for AI coworkers (donvitocodes.com/coworker).
  • The creator thinks Grok Bot already solves the personal/work AI assistant use case.
  • How Coworker stands apart: it is model-agnostic, so it works with any LLM, local or hosted.
  • Product lesson: when a big platform ships a similar assistant, lean on what you do differently (local-first, any model, cheaper).
