# CEO AI Brain: agents answer business questions with charts

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-06-26 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2070223044916990326)

**Topics:** AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, Portfolio Projects · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

The creator is building a 'CEO AI Brain': AI agents that answer business questions straight from the company database, so no meetings are needed. He says the answers look better with charts in Claude Desktop. The idea is still being refined.

## Key points

- Idea: AI agents answer a CEO's business questions straight from the database.
- The goal is no meetings and no human in the loop: a team made only of AI.
- Showing answers as charts in Claude Desktop made them easier to read.
- The project is still a work in progress.
