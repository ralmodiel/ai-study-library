Where Grok Bot stores your private Skills and how to edit them

Summary

Grok Bot keeps your private Skills (workflows) as files on its own sandboxed computer. You can ask the bot to create or update them, or open its computer and edit the files by hand.

Key points

  • Grok Bot saves private Skills in the folder /home/box/agent-data/workflows on its own machine.
  • Skills can be created or updated by asking Grok Bot in chat.
  • You can also open the agent's computer and edit Skill files manually.
  • Because Skills are stored as files, you can inspect and change agent behavior directly.
