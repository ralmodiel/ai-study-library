# Personal Assistant Agent on Hermes: Morning Briefings and Inbox Triage

## Summary

Melvin Vivas shows a personal assistant he built on the open-source Hermes agent. It runs on his own setup with no subscriptions. Each morning it sends a briefing, and during the day it sorts his email and handles family tasks. The caption says the video itself was also made by the Hermes agent: it used Hyperframes with the Grok 4.5 model and finished in about 10 minutes.

## Key points

- The morning briefing includes the calendar, overnight messages, the top three priorities, and a list of things that can safely be ignored today.
- Inbox triage sorts email into three piles: needs me now, can wait, and ignore.
- Human-in-the-loop: the agent drafts replies but never sends them without the user's approval.
- The same agent runs the family grocery list and the kids' schedule.
- His test for usability: his non-technical wife uses it.
- It's built on the open-source Hermes agent, self-hosted, with no subscriptions.
- Per the caption, the agent made this video with Hyperframes and Grok 4.5 in about 10 minutes.
- The full build walkthrough is on the creator's channel.
