## Key points

- An AI agent setup the creator calls "Hermes" produces most of the explainer video for him.
- Only two steps are still manual: adding background music in CapCut and adding the outro.
- The creator says this is a Hermes use case he has been using a lot lately, so it is part of his regular workflow.
- No prompts, configuration or pipeline details are given. The video is a demo, not a tutorial.
- A practical pattern: let AI do the repetitive production work and do the creative finishing (music, branding, outro) by hand in a normal editor.
