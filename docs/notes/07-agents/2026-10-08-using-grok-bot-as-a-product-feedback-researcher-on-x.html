Summary

Melvin plans to update his 'X Researcher' Grok Bot. The quoted post says Grok Bot can search X for all feedback on what you're building, summarize it and propose a plan to address it, without needing an X account or connector.

Key points

  • Grok Bot can search X directly; no X account or connector setup is needed.
  • Workflow: ask the bot to find all feedback about your product, summarize it, then propose an action plan.
  • The creator runs a dedicated 'X Researcher' bot for this kind of task.
