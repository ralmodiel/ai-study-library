## Summary

The creator agrees with a quoted post saying engineering, product, design and data science are merging into new kinds of roles. The quoted author sees five archetypes on the Claude Code team, starting with the 'Prototyper'. The creator thinks the best people will do more than one role.

## Key points

- AI lets engineers, designers, PMs and growth people move into each other's work.
- The best people will probably cover more than one role.
- The quoted post describes five role archetypes seen on the Claude Code team.
- Archetype 1, 'Prototyper': comes up with brand-new ideas and builds them quickly.
- Career takeaway: build skills beyond your core function.
