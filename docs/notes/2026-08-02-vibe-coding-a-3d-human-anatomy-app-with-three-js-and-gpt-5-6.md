## Summary

Melvin shares a 3D human anatomy learning app that someone vibe-coded with Three.js and GPT 5.6 Sol, starting from a single design image. It shows how AI coding can produce interactive educational tools.

## Key points

- The app was built through vibe coding with GPT 5.6 Sol (ChatGPT).
- Three.js handles the 3D rendering.
- It started from one design image the author made.
- Interactive 3D apps are a strong format for education.
