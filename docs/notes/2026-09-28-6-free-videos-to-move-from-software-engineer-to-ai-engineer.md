# 6 Free Videos to Move from Software Engineer to AI Engineer

Bashiri Smith · Facebook reel · 2026-09-28 · 0:55 · 7,788 views · [Open on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1615291686990735)

**Topics:** Start Here: Roadmaps & Strategy, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Bashiri Smith suggests watching six free videos in a set order instead of TV after work. The order goes from LLM basics to embeddings, then RAG, then AI agents and agentic design patterns. It ends with his own Complete AI Engineer Roadmap video, which lists the exact courses to take step by step toward an AI engineering role.

## Key points

- Step 1: Andrej Karpathy's 'Intro to Large Language Models' explains what LLMs are, how they work, and why they matter to software engineers.
- Step 2: StatQuest's 'Word Embedding and Word2Vec, Clearly Explained' covers embeddings and semantic search, which you need before RAG.
- Step 3: freeCodeCamp's 'RAG from Scratch' teaches the basics of RAG. He calls it the AI engineering skill employers ask for most.
- Step 4: IBM's 'What Are AI Agents' covers agent fundamentals.
- Step 5: Mark Kashef's 'Master All 20 Agentic Design Patterns' teaches core patterns you can build into your own agents.
- Step 6: The creator's 'Complete AI Engineer Roadmap' gives the exact courses to take in order.
- Suggested order: LLMs, then embeddings, then RAG, then agents, then agent patterns, then the full roadmap.
- He says this path can lead to a $100k–$200k+ AI engineering role.
