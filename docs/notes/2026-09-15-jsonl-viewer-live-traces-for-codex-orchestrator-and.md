# jsonl-viewer: Live Traces for Codex Orchestrator and Subagents

Melvin Vivas · X video post · 2026-09-15 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2099753117504172186)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, Fine-tuning & Model Customization · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

The creator added a traces view to his open-source jsonl-viewer. He built it to check his Codex orchestrator/subagent setup. While Codex runs, you can watch live agent traces and open its subagents. The tool also views and edits .jsonl files, such as fine-tuning datasets.

## Key points

- jsonl-viewer views and edits .jsonl files, often used for fine-tuning/training datasets
- The new traces view shows live Codex agent traces while it runs
- You can drill into subagents to check an orchestrator/subagent setup
