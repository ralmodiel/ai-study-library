# Claude Chat and Cowork Merge into One Conversation

## Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares Anthropic's announcement that Claude Chat and Claude Cowork are becoming one Claude experience. In the video, Megan Choi, design lead for Claude apps, explains that you no longer choose a mode first. You can ask a quick question or hand over a bigger multi-step task in the same conversation, and Claude brings in whatever the task needs. It's a product update, not a tutorial, but it shows that chat assistants and agents are merging into one interface.

## Key points

- Chat and Cowork are now one conversation, so you don't have to decide where a task belongs before you start.
- Cowork was where Anthropic worked out how Claude should take on bigger, multi-step work. Claude got good enough at deciding what a task needs that a separate space no longer made sense.
- Claude keeps working on a handed-off task even after you close your laptop. If something is unclear it asks you, and you keep the final say.
- Your existing chats, tasks, skills and memories carry over unchanged.
- Example use: give it last week's numbers and have it draft a deck or other visuals right in the conversation.
- Every output stays yours to keep, edit or steer.
- It's rolling out to Pro and Max plans over the next few weeks. Anthropic calls it a 'first step' and says the experience will keep improving as the models get better.
