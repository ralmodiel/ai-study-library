# GLM 5.2 vs Opus 4.8 for Landing Page Design: 6x Cheaper

Melvin Vivas · X video post · 2026-06-19 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2067710443419005016)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

A side-by-side test built a landing page with GLM 5.2 and with Claude Opus 4.8. The results look almost the same. GLM cost $0.06 and Opus cost $0.49, and GLM was also faster and used fewer tokens.

## Key points

- GLM 5.2 and Opus 4.8 produced landing pages that are hard to tell apart
- Cost: GLM $0.06 vs Opus $0.49, more than 6x cheaper
- GLM was faster and more token-efficient
- Open models can be cost-effective for UI and design tasks, so compare models on your own task
