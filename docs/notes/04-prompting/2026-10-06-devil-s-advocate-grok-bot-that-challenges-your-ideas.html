Devil's Advocate Grok Bot That Challenges Your Ideas

Summary

The creator shares a Grok Bot template called Devil's Advocate. It uses Socratic questioning instead of praise, so weak assumptions in a business offer or idea get challenged before you commit. This is a good example of using a system prompt to stop an AI from just agreeing with you.

Key points

  • Set up the bot to push back on your ideas instead of praising them.
  • It uses Socratic questioning to expose weak assumptions.
  • It helps you shape an offer or idea before you commit to it.
  • The bot is shared as a reusable public link.

  • Try the Devil's Advocate bot on an idea or offer before you commit to it.
  • Build your own critic bot whose prompt tells it to question assumptions instead of agreeing.
