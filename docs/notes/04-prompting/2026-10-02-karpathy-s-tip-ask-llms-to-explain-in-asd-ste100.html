Karpathy's Tip: Ask LLMs to Explain in ASD-STE100

Summary

Shares Andrej Karpathy's tips for understanding LLM outputs. One writing trick: ask the LLM to explain a topic in ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English), a controlled language specification. This gives clearer, more consistent explanations.

Key points

  • We will spend more and more time reading and understanding LLM outputs.
  • Writing trick: ask your LLM to explain something in ASD-STE100.
  • ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English) is a controlled language specification with limited vocabulary and strict writing rules.
  • Constraining the output language makes explanations simpler and easier to understand.
