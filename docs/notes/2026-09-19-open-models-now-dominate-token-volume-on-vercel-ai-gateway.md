# Open models now dominate token volume on Vercel AI Gateway

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-19 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2101188319703134335)

**Topics:** Industry Trends & Job Market, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, LLMOps, Deployment & Monitoring · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

The creator argues for using open models more to keep costs sustainable, and for improving agent harnesses with Skills to power workflows. He quotes Vercel AI Gateway stats: open models had a record 78.4% share of token volume. Moonshot AI, DeepSeek and Z.ai together outspent OpenAI that day.

## Key points

- On Vercel AI Gateway, open models reached 78.4% of token volume vs 21.6% for closed models on a record day.
- By spend, Moonshot AI and DeepSeek ranked #3 and #4; together with Z.ai, their combined spend passed OpenAI (#2).
- Token volume and spend tell different stories: open models are used heavily but cost less.
- The creator recommends moving more workloads to open models to keep costs sustainable.
- Improve agent harnesses with reusable Skills so cheaper models can still run strong workflows.
