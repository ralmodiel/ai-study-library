# Google AI Pro/Ultra Subscriptions Now Work in Google AI Studio

## Summary

This short post shares news that Google AI Pro and Ultra subscriptions now work inside Google AI Studio. Subscribers can vibe code and use the AI Studio playground with higher rate limits. The video has no speech, so the caption is the whole lesson: if you already pay for Google AI, you can prototype with Gemini models in AI Studio and get more usage.

## Key points

- Google AI subscriptions (Pro and Ultra) can now be used in Google AI Studio.
- Subscribers get higher rate limits in the AI Studio playground and its vibe-coding (app-building) feature.
- Melvin Vivas points out that this lets him put his existing Google AI subscription to work in AI Studio.
- The quoted announcement says the feature was available right away (April 2026).
- Google AI Studio is a browser tool for testing Gemini prompts and models and for vibe coding app prototypes.
