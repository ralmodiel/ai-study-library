# A Day of Coding with Claude Fable 5 in Cursor: First Impressions

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-07-03 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2072766651247075450)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, LLM Fundamentals · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator used only Claude Fable 5 in Cursor for a full workday. He found it slow but with very little back-and-forth and not chatty. It produced PRs good enough to merge, and he deployed them to production the same day.

## Key points

- Claude Fable 5 is slow to respond.
- It needs very little back-and-forth to finish a task.
- Its output is concise, not chatty.
- Its PRs were good enough to merge and were deployed to production the same day.
- The creator expects a big productivity boost from this model.
