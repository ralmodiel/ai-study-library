# MiniMax 2.7 One-Shots a Linear Clone at 95% Lower Cost

Melvin Vivas · X video post · 2026-03-31 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2038678970288513057)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, AI Dev Tools & Productivity, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Sherry Jiang used MiniMax 2.7 to build a Linear clone in one shot in about 10 minutes. She says it cost about 95% less than Claude Opus 4.6. Her lesson: compare models yourself instead of defaulting to whatever is hyped, or you may overpay for the same result.

## Key points

- MiniMax 2.7 one-shot a Linear (issue tracker) clone in about 10 minutes.
- The claimed cost was about 95% cheaper than Claude Opus 4.6.
- Cheaper, less-hyped models can match frontier models on some app-building tasks.
- Benchmark models on your own tasks to control cost.
