Summary

Google released two generative media models for developers and enterprises, both pitched on cost-performance. Nano Banana 2 Lite is the fastest and cheapest image model in the Nano Banana family. Gemini Omni Flash is a natively multimodal model aimed at high quality at low cost.

Key points

  • Nano Banana 2 Lite: the fastest, most cost-efficient image model in the Nano Banana family
  • Gemini Omni Flash: a natively multimodal model focused on high quality at low cost
  • Both are aimed at developers and enterprises, with cost-performance as the main pitch
