Qwen3.8-Max announced with open weights for Max and 27B

Summary

The creator shares Qwen's announcement of Qwen3.8-Max, its most capable model so far, with 2.4T parameters aimed at coding and cowork. The open weights of Qwen3.8-Max and Qwen3.8-27B are due the following week. The creator is waiting for the 27B to run locally.

Key points

  • Qwen3.8-Max has 2.4T parameters and targets coding and cowork tasks.
  • The announcement lists autonomous coding as a highlight (the quoted text is cut off at '10+ days of').
  • Open weights for Qwen3.8-Max are due the following week.
  • Qwen3.8-27B is also going open-weights, which makes it suitable for running locally.
