Summary

A news item reports that the US government asked OpenAI to stagger the GPT-5.6 release, with access approved customer by customer. The creator argues that the rest of the world should get used to open models. The lesson is about the risk of depending on a single closed model provider.

Key points

  • Reported: the US administration asked OpenAI to stagger the GPT-5.6 release over security concerns.
  • Reported: the government will approve access to GPT-5.6 customer by customer.
  • Creator's view: teams outside the US should get comfortable with open models.
  • Takeaway: plan for frontier closed models being restricted, for example by keeping open-model fallbacks.
