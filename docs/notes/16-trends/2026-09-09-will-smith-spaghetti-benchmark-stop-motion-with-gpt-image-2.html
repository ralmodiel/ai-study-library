Summary

A 17-second demo with no narration. It shows a new take on the informal "Will Smith eating spaghetti" benchmark, a meme used to track how much AI image and video generation has improved. The creator made a stop-motion clip by generating frames with GPT Image 2.5 and putting them together with a tool called Astra. It shows what current tools can do, but it doesn't explain how the clip was made.

Key points

  • "Will Smith eating spaghetti" is a community meme benchmark. People use it to compare AI image and video generation quality over time.
  • This version is a stop-motion clip, not a video generated directly from a prompt.
  • The frames were made with GPT Image 2.5, the image model named in the quoted post.
  • Astra was used with GPT Image 2.5 to turn the frames into the stop-motion clip. The post doesn't explain exactly what Astra did.
  • The clip has no speech or tutorial. It's a showcase of what the tools can do, not a how-to.
