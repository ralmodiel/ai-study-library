ComfyUI Raises $30M: Open-Source Visual AI Pipelines Move into Production

Summary

Melvin Vivas reposts ComfyUI's funding announcement video, captioned only with "great". ComfyUI raised $30M at a $500M valuation, led by Craft Ventures. The video presents ComfyUI as an open-source, node-based engine for building reproducible image, video, audio and 3D generation pipelines that studios use in production. It's mostly company news, but it shows how open-source generative tools are being adopted and that "ComfyUI Artist" now shows up as a job title.

Key points

  • Funding: $30M raised at a $500M valuation. The post says this brings total funding to $47M, but the video says $48M. Craft Ventures led the round, with Pace Capital, Chemistry, TruArrow and others.
  • ComfyUI is an open-source, node-based visual workflow engine for generative AI across images, video, audio and 3D.
  • Workflows cover the whole pipeline around a model, not just the model itself. You control the parameters and can reproduce the output, which matters when moving from experiments to production.
  • The company claims more than 4M users, over 5,000 extensions and 60,000 community-built custom nodes. It says new models from any lab often run in ComfyUI within hours of release.
  • It says teams at Netflix, Apple, Amazon Studios and Ubisoft build production pipelines on it. VFX teams connect it to Nuke, Blender and Houdini.
  • Trend: companies now screen for ComfyUI skills, and "ComfyUI Artist" appears as a job title on job boards.
  • The money will go to team collaboration tools, more cloud infrastructure and new modalities.
  • Core argument: open source is the only architecture that can keep up with how fast AI moves.
