Why Chinese AI Models Are Mostly Open Source

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 20 July 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

Melvin Vivas connects the open release of many Chinese models (GLM, Kimi, Qwen, DeepSeek) to China's stated policy of international AI cooperation. He quotes Xi Jinping and links a CNA news video on China positioning itself as a leader of a new global AI order.

Key points

  • Major Chinese model families GLM, Kimi, Qwen and DeepSeek are mostly released as open source or open weights.
  • Xi Jinping: AI development should be 'a symphony of international cooperation', not a solo performance by one country.
  • China presents open AI cooperation as a challenge to US dominance.
