Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares Clueso's announcement of a $10M Series A for its AI video-creation platform. He argues that automating video creation is a large market that brands and content creators need. The attached video is a comedy skit in a mock-documentary style. It shows the problems Clueso says it solves: product UI changes that make tutorial videos outdated, the repetitive work of recording personalized sales videos, and expensive software that nobody uses because there are no training videos. It's mostly a funding announcement and product promo, with no technical lesson.

Key points

  • Clueso raised a $10M Series A to automate video creation for companies.
  • The company says 1,000+ organizations use it, including Atlassian, GitLab, Samsara, Legrand and Zendesk.
  • Its target uses are marketing, customer education (product tutorials) and employee training or compliance videos.
  • Problem shown in the skit: a small UI change (e.g., rounded buttons) makes hundreds of product tutorial videos outdated and they have to be re-recorded.
  • Problem shown in the skit: personalized sales outreach videos are slow and repetitive to record by hand at scale.
  • Problem shown in the skit: costly software (the Salesforce example) goes unused when there are no training videos.
  • The creator's opinion: automating video creation with AI is a huge market.
