# Liquid AI's LFM2-Longevity models for aging-data analysis

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-18 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2100626614128378309)

**Topics:** Industry Trends & Job Market, Fine-tuning & Model Customization · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

Liquid AI released LFM2-1.2B-Longevity and LFM2-2.6B-Longevity, small models built to interpret structured aging data. They cover several data types: clinical records, DNA methylation, transcriptomics, proteomics and genetics. The release is a good example of small, domain-specialized models.

## Key points

- Two sizes were released: LFM2-1.2B-Longevity and LFM2-2.6B-Longevity.
- They are specialized for interpreting structured aging data across different data types.
- Clinical records come from NHANES.
- DNA methylation data comes from GEO.
- Transcriptomics data comes from GTEx.
- Proteomics data comes from Olink; genetic data is also covered.
