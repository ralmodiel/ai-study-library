Ideogram 4.0 Released as an Open-Weights Image Model

This note summarises a video post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 10 June 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

Melvin Vivas shares news that Ideogram has open-sourced its image model. The quoted announcement calls Ideogram 4.0 "the best open image model in the world." It says you can download the weights, fine-tune the model on your own data and run it on your own hardware. It is also available on every Ideogram plan and through the API. The post is a news item about the trend toward open image models. It contains no tutorial.

Key points

  • Ideogram 4.0 is released with open, downloadable model weights.
  • Ideogram claims it is 'the best open image model in the world'. This is the company's own claim and no benchmark is shown.
  • Because the weights are open, you can fine-tune the model on your own data, for example with LoRA-style customization.
  • You can run the model on your own hardware, which helps with privacy, cost control and ownership.
  • The hosted version is live on every Ideogram plan and through the Ideogram API. The post says nothing about pricing or the license terms.
  • The video has no speech. All the information comes from the caption and the quoted announcement.
