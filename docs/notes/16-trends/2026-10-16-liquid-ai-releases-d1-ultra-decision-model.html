Summary

The creator says Liquid AI is shipping new models faster than he can update his notebooks. The quoted post announces d1-ultra, a multimodal 'decision model' that supports 44 languages and ranks #1 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index. It's a pointer to a fast-moving model family worth tracking.

Key points

  • Liquid AI released d1-ultra, which it calls its most powerful decision model, built for hard decision problems
  • d1-ultra is multimodal and supports 44 languages
  • Claimed #1 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index with a score of 65
  • Claimed to lead all eight categories of Liquid AI's own 112-evaluation suite
  • The creator keeps notebooks that support Liquid AI's decision models and has a backlog to update
