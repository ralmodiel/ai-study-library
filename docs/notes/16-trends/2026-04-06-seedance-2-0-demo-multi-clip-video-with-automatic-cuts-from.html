Summary

This is a 15-second demo with no speech, shared by Melvin Vivas. It reposts a test showing ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 video model, used through Dreamina, making a video with several clips and the cuts between them in a single generation. It's a quick look at where AI video generation is heading. It doesn't teach how to do it.

Key points

  • Seedance 2.0 can produce a video made of several clips in one generation. You don't have to make each shot separately.
  • According to the quoted post, the model chooses the clips and makes the cuts between them by itself.
  • The test was run on Dreamina (@dreamina_ai), the platform where Seedance is offered.
  • The hashtag #dreaminacpp suggests the quoted post is part of a Dreamina creator program, so it may be promotional.
  • The post doesn't share the prompt, settings, cost or any limitations. It only shows what the model can do.
