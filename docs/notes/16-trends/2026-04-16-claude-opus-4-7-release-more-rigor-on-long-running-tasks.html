The creator reacts to Anthropic's launch of Claude Opus 4.7 and wants his Claude Max subscription back. The quoted announcement says the model handles long-running tasks with more rigor, follows instructions more precisely and checks its own outputs before reporting back.

  • It is said to follow instructions more precisely.
  • It checks its own outputs before reporting back.
