## Summary

Melvin Vivas shares Google's launch of the Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash-Lite TTS text-to-speech models. Listed features include voice design, 2,000+ production-ready voices, voice replication, support for 100 languages, upcoming voice remixing, and the #1 spot on Hume AI's voice benchmarks.

## Key points

- Two new TTS models: Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS.
- A new voice design experience.
- 2,000+ production-ready voices.
- Voice replication, plus voice remixing coming soon.
- Support for 100 languages.
- Claimed #1 spot on Hume AI's voice benchmarks.
