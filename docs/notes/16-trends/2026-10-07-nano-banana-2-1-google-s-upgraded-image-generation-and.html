Summary

The creator shares Google's announcement of Nano Banana 2.1, a new image generation and editing model. Google says it beats its earlier models across the board, with big improvements in visual design, mask-based editing and keeping subjects consistent.

Key points

  • Nano Banana 2.1 is Google's newest image generation and editing model.
  • Google says it beats its earlier image models across the board.
  • The biggest improvements are in visual design quality.
  • Mask-based editing is better, so you can edit specific regions of an image more precisely.
  • Subject consistency is stronger, so the same subject looks the same across images and the results look more natural.
