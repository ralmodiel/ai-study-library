Summary

Links a CNBC article reporting that Alibaba banned Anthropic's AI for its employees after an accusation of a 'distillation attack'. The story is relevant to industry trends and to concerns about model distillation and IP.

Key points

  • Alibaba banned its employees from using Anthropic's Claude.
  • The ban followed an accusation of a 'distillation attack', which means using a model's outputs to train a competing model.
  • Shows growing geopolitical and IP tension around frontier model access.
