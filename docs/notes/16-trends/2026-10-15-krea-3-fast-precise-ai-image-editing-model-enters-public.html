Summary

Melvin Vivas shares the launch of Krea 3, a new image-editing model now in public beta. According to the announcement, it makes 2K edits from start to finish in under 8 seconds, with quality said to match Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2.5. It also offers bounding-box placement and multi-turn edits that change only the pixels that need to change. This is product news, useful for seeing where low-latency image editing is heading and what it could power, not a tutorial.

Key points

  • Krea 3 is an image-editing model, and its public beta opened in October 2026.
  • Speed claim: complete 2K-resolution edits in under 8 seconds.
  • Quality claim: on par with Google's Nano Banana Pro and OpenAI's GPT Image 2.5.
  • Bounding-box control lets you decide where each element goes in a scene.
  • Edits are local: only the pixels that need changing are modified, and the rest of the image stays the same. Multi-turn edits are supported.
  • Supports up to 4K resolution and keeps characters and products consistent (a person's or product's visual features are preserved).
  • Suggested uses that need speed: personalized shopping and voice-driven editing.
  • Planned features: style references, LoRAs and open-source models.
