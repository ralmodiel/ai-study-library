Summary

Industry news: SpaceXAI and Cursor are working together on AI for coding and knowledge work. The deal combines Cursor's product and its reach among software engineers with SpaceX's Colossus training supercomputer. The creator notes that SpaceX has the right to buy Cursor.

Key points

  • SpaceXAI and Cursor announced a close partnership on coding and knowledge-work AI.
  • Cursor brings the product and its distribution to expert software engineers.
  • SpaceX brings the Colossus training supercomputer, described as about a million H100 equivalents.
  • According to the creator, SpaceX has the right to buy Cursor.
