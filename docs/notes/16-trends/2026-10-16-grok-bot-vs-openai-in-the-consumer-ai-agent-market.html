Summary

The creator thinks that Grok Bot has put SpaceX/xAI ahead of OpenAI in consumer AI agents. He says the OpenClaw phenomenon already showed big demand for personal agents. He is surprised OpenAI didn't take that lead after hiring "Pete".

Key points

  • Consumer-facing AI agents are becoming a contested market.
  • The creator thinks Grok Bot has an early lead over OpenAI in consumer agents.
  • OpenClaw's popularity is cited as proof of large demand for personal AI agents.
  • This is opinion and market commentary, not a tutorial.
