Summary

The creator quotes a16z's map of consumer AI. It names early winners in search, creative tools and productivity, and says winners are still missing in social, marketplaces, travel, finance, hiring, dating, gaming and media. The creator adds that he thinks Grok Bot should be leading consumer AI.

Key points

  • a16z sees early consumer AI winners in search, creative tools and productivity.
  • It sees no clear winners yet in social, marketplaces, travel, finance, hiring, dating, gaming and media.
  • These open categories are possible opportunities for startups and side projects.

Resources mentioned

  • a16z (Andreessen Horowitz) · website · a16z.com · free
    A venture capital firm that publishes maps and analysis of the consumer AI market.

Try this

  • Build a consumer AI app in an open category, such as travel, finance or hiring.
