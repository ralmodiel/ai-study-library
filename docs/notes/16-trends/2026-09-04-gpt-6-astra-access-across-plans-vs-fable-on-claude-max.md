## Summary

The creator notes that GPT-6 Astra will be included in every plan's normal usage allocation. He contrasts this with Fable, which he says requires a Claude Max subscription. It's a comparison of how frontier models are made available.

## Key points

- According to the quoted post, Astra is included in the normal usage allocation on every plan.
- Users can put 100% of their allocation toward Astra.
- According to the creator, Fable requires a Claude Max subscription.
- Plan-gated access is a practical factor when choosing which frontier model to use.
