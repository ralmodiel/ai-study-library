DeepSWE results: GPT-6 Sol slightly below GPT-5.6 Sol, but cheaper

Summary

Shares DeepSWE benchmark results showing that GPT-6 Sol peaks slightly below its predecessor GPT-5.6 Sol on coding tasks, though it costs less. GPT-6 Luna is slightly better and cheaper than GPT-5.6 Luna, but it isn't a big jump. The lesson: a newer model version isn't automatically better, so compare benchmarks and cost for your own use case.

Key points

  • On DeepSWE, GPT-6 Sol peaks slightly below GPT-5.6 Sol.
  • GPT-6 Sol is cheaper than GPT-5.6 Sol.
  • GPT-6 Luna improves slightly over GPT-5.6 Luna and is also cheaper.
  • The generational gain is small, so a newer version doesn't guarantee better coding performance.
  • When choosing a coding model, weigh benchmark scores against cost per task.
