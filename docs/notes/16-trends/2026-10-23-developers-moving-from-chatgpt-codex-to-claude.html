Developers moving from ChatGPT/Codex to Claude

Summary

An opinion post: developers are moving from OpenAI (ChatGPT, Codex) to Claude. The reasons given are poor service availability and slow inference, not model quality. It shows that reliability and latency matter when choosing a provider.

Key points

  • The quoted user cancelled 8 ChatGPT subscriptions because of poor availability and very slow inference.
  • The creator says Codex hasn't been worth it since DevDay.
  • Provider reliability and latency can matter more than model quality.
