Summary

A news roundup that points to the creator's AI news page. It covers three releases: SpaceXAI's Grok 4.5 for coding, agents and knowledge work; OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna (public launch July 9); and OpenAI's GPT-Live full-duplex voice models for ChatGPT Voice.

Key points

  • SpaceXAI launched Grok 4.5, aimed at coding, agents and knowledge work
  • OpenAI confirmed the public launch of GPT-5.6 in three variants (Sol, Terra, Luna) on July 9
  • OpenAI launched GPT-Live, full-duplex voice models (the model can listen and speak at the same time) for ChatGPT Voice
  • donvitocodes.com/ai-news collects daily AI announcements and model releases
