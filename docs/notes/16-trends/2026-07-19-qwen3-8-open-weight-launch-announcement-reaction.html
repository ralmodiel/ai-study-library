Summary

Melvin Vivas reacts to the announcement that Qwen3.8 is coming soon. The quoted post says it has 2.4T parameters, will be released with open weights, and ranks second only to Fable 5. These are the vendor's own claims, not verified results.

Key points

  • Qwen3.8: about 2.4T parameters, open weights to follow.
  • Qwen says it is comparable to frontier models and second only to Fable 5.
  • Qwen says the model is continuously evolving.
