Summary

OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna. They build on GPT-6 Astra and are faster, cheaper models for work at scale. OpenAI also says it made caching and inference more efficient. The creator sees this as OpenAI answering competitors and jokes about wanting a usage-limit reset.

Key points

  • GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna are new additions to OpenAI's GPT-6 family.
  • They build on GPT-6 Astra's advances and bring its strengths to faster, more affordable models.
  • They are positioned for work at scale.
  • OpenAI also made caching and inference more efficient.
  • The creator frames the release as OpenAI 'fighting back' in the model race.
