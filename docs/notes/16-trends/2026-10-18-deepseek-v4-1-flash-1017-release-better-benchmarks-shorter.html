Summary

Shares DeepSeek's announcement of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017. DeepSeek says it scores higher on benchmarks, finishes tasks faster and gives shorter outputs than the previous version, so it improves on both performance and cost. It is live on DeepSeek's website, app and API, and tuned for agent harnesses such as DeepSeek Harness and Tencent CodeBuddy.

Key points

  • New model: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017.
  • DeepSeek claims stronger benchmark results, faster task completion and shorter outputs than the previous version.
  • DeepSeek calls it a Pareto improvement: better performance at lower cost, since shorter outputs mean fewer tokens.
  • Available on the DeepSeek website, app and API.
  • Optimized for coding/agent harnesses including DeepSeek Harness and Tencent CodeBuddy.
