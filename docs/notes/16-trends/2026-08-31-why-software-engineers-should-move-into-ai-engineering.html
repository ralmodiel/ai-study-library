Summary

Bashiri Smith argues that AI is changing software engineering more than any other profession, but job openings are still going up. The catch is that engineers who still code like it's 2022 will struggle. He presents AI engineering as the best skill to add: it pays more, it is growing fast, and most of it overlaps with what software engineers already do. The video ends with a pitch for his AI Engineer community.

Key points

  • An unnamed 'AI expert' is quoted saying software engineering is the profession AI affects most right now.
  • Despite the 'doom fearmongering', software engineering job openings are up.
  • Engineers who still write code 'like it's 2022' are in trouble and need new skills.
  • According to LinkedIn, AI Engineer is the #1 fastest-growing job in the U.S.
  • AI Engineers earn about 20% more than Software Engineers on average.
  • About 80% of an AI Engineer's job is work software engineers already do, so your existing fundamentals give you a big head start.
  • The creator's community offers an AI engineering roadmap, daily calls with AI and ML engineers, and guidance from recruiters.
