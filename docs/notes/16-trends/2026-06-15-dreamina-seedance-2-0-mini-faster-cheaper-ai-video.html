Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares the launch of Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Mini, a lower-cost, faster version of the Seedance 2.0 video generation model, adding only "faster and cheaper!!!" The quoted post says Mini costs about 30% less than Seedance 2.0, runs 2× faster than Seedance 2.0 Fast, and produces output comparable to Seedance 2.0 Fast. This is product news; the 10-second video teaches nothing beyond these launch figures.

Key points

  • Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Mini is a new video generation model variant built for quality and speed at a lower cost.
  • It costs about 30% less than Dreamina Seedance 2.0.
  • It is about 2× faster than Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Fast.
  • Its output quality is described as comparable to Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Fast.
  • The launch included offers for Pro subscribers, but the caption is cut off before the details.
  • Takeaway: AI video model families now come in several tiers (standard, Fast, Mini), so you can trade off cost, speed and quality.
