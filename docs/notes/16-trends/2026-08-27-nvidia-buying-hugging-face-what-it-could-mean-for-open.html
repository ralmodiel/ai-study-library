NVIDIA buying Hugging Face: what it could mean for open models

Summary

The creator reacts to news that NVIDIA is buying Hugging Face. He guesses it could lead to tighter links between NVIDIA hardware and open models, such as small language models (SLMs) packaged for DGX Spark, plus combined tools for running models (inference) and training them further after release (post-training). These are guesses, not confirmed plans.

Key points

  • Reported news: NVIDIA is acquiring Hugging Face, the main hub for open models.
  • Guess: SLMs could come pre-packaged on NVIDIA DGX Spark machines.
  • Hoped-for outcome: tight integration between NVIDIA hardware and Hugging Face tools for inference and post-training.
  • Trend to watch: hardware vendors joining up with open-model platforms.
