Grok Bot's Real Competitors: OpenClaw and Hermes

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 6 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-10-06 · Open on X

Topics: Industry Trends & Job Market, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP · Level: beginner

Summary

The creator's opinion is that, based on assistant features, Grok Bot competes with personal-agent tools like OpenClaw and Hermes rather than with OpenAI's Dots. The post is useful as a quick map of the personal AI assistant market.

Key points

  • Compare assistant products by their features, not by which company makes them.
  • The creator sees Grok Bot's main competitors as OpenClaw and Hermes, not Dots.
