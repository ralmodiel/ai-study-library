Summary

A short demo video quote-posted from an OpenAI announcement about GPT-Live-1.1. The update adds new voices and better accents, and the model says "checking..." less often. It covers Voice in ChatGPT, Codex and "dots", with new voices in French, German, Hindi, Mexican Spanish, Korean and Japanese. The creator's only comment is that "everyone is running after Duolingo", meaning AI voice could compete with language-learning apps. There is no technical lesson here. It is product news that shows where real-time voice AI is heading.

Key points

  • GPT-Live-1.1 is a voice model update that brings new voices, better accents and fewer "checking..." pauses.
  • It applies to Voice in ChatGPT, Codex and "dots".
  • New voices: French, German, Hindi, Mexican Spanish, Korean and Japanese.
  • It is rolling out to all users now. API access is "coming soon", so developers can't build on it yet.
  • In the demo, voices switch between several languages in one conversation, including code-switching mid-sentence.
  • The creator thinks multilingual voice AI could compete with language-learning apps like Duolingo.
