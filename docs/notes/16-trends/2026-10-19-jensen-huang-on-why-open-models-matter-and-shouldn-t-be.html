Jensen Huang on Why Open Models Matter and Shouldn't Be Regulated Away

Summary

This short clip, shared by Melvin Vivas, shows NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang explaining why he publicly defended open AI models. He rejects the idea that closed models are safer than open ones, calling it "exactly the opposite." He says open models will be the base for entire new industries and should not be regulated out of existence. The clip is an opinion on industry and policy, not a technical tutorial.

Key points

  • Some movements around the world have argued that closed models are safe and open models are unsafe. Huang says it is "exactly the opposite."
  • Huang says he usually avoids speaking out ("I'm the turtle") but felt this issue was too important to stay quiet on.
  • His main claim is that open models are foundational to entire industries that have yet to be created.
  • His policy position is that open models must not be regulated out of existence.
  • The quoted post adds that developers need the freedom to build on open models and that keeping them open and accessible matters.
  • For learners, this is useful background on the open vs. closed model debate, which affects which models you can freely build on, self-host or fine-tune.
