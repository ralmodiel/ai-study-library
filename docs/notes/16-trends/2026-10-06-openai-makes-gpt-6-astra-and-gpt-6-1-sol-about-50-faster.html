Summary

A quoted OpenAI post says default speed is now about 50% faster for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol. This applies to all products and to partner tools that use 'Sign in With ChatGPT', such as OpenCode, Pi, Amp and Devin. The creator says GPT-6.1 Sol was unavailable when they tried it, so new rollouts may not be reliable at first.

Key points

  • Default speed is about 50% faster for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol on the subscription.
  • The change also applies to partner tools that use Sign in With ChatGPT: OpenCode, Pi, Amp, Devin.
  • Users don't need to change anything.
  • The creator saw the model unavailable, so expect reliability problems during rollouts.
