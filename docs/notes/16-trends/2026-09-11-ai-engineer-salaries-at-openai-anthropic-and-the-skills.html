Summary

The video points to two Applied AI Engineer job postings, one at Anthropic ($200K–$320K) and one at OpenAI ($230K–$385K+), as evidence that companies pay well for engineers who turn AI into real products. It names the skills the postings ask for: building agents, working with LLMs, writing evaluations, and getting AI systems to run reliably in production. It also says LinkedIn ranked AI engineer as the fastest-growing role in the US in 2026, and suggests that people who already code turn these skills into projects they can explain in interviews. The video ends with a pitch for the creator's coaching community.

Key points

  • Anthropic Applied AI Engineer role: $200,000–$320,000 per year.
  • OpenAI Applied AI Engineer role: $230,000–$385,000+ per year.
  • Companies are paying for engineers who can turn AI into actual products, not just research.
  • LinkedIn ranked AI engineer as the #1 fastest-growing role in the US in 2026.
  • Skills listed in the job postings: building agents, working with LLMs, creating evaluations, and shipping reliable AI systems to production.
  • Already knowing how to code gives you a strong base for moving into AI engineering.
  • Build these skills into portfolio projects you can explain clearly in an interview.
