Summary

A quoted post says Jensen Huang and Satya Nadella shared a joint letter, released by several companies and organizations at once, urging policymakers not to restrict open-weight models. The creator points out that OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI did not sign it.

Key points

  • Several companies and organizations released a joint letter urging policymakers not to restrict open-weight models.
  • NVIDIA's Jensen Huang and Microsoft's Satya Nadella both shared it.
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI did not sign.
  • This shows a policy split between closed frontier labs and open-weight supporters.
