The creator says GPT 5.6 Luna gives the best price-to-performance. He suggests using Luna instead of the larger Sol model in Codex so your usage limits last longer.

Key points

  • The creator rates GPT 5.6 Luna best on price versus performance.
  • Defaulting to Luna instead of Sol in Codex stretches your usage limits.
  • General lesson: pick the cheapest model that is good enough for the task, not always the biggest one.
