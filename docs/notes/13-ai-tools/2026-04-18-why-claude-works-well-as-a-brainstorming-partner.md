# Why Claude Works Well as a Brainstorming Partner

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-04-18 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2045404254375157902)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator says Claude is currently the best app for brainstorming and lists the features behind that view. In his opinion, ChatGPT and Gemini are not close.

## Key points

- Claude makes good use of memory across conversations.
- It asks clarifying questions before going ahead.
- It explains concepts thoroughly.
- It can visualize concepts when you ask it to.
- It uses Skills when it needs to.
- The creator ranks it above ChatGPT and Gemini for brainstorming.
