## Summary

News: GLM 5.3 and GLM 5.3 Flash can now be used in Cursor. GLM 5.3 Max is called the best-scoring open-weight model on CursorBench 4.0, so it is worth trying as an open-weight coding model.

## Key points

- GLM 5.3 and GLM 5.3 Flash can now be picked in Cursor.
- GLM 5.3 Max is reported as the top open-weight model on CursorBench 4.0.
- Flash is the faster, lighter version.
