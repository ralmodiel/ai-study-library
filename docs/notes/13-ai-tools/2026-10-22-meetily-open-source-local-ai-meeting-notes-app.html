Meetily: Open-Source Local AI Meeting Notes App

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 22 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

The creator shares Meetily, a free, open-source AI meeting-notes app that runs locally. It uses Whisper and Parakeet models to transcribe audio and local Qwen and Gemma models to summarize. The creator plans to try connecting it to Grok Bot.

Key points

  • Meetily is free and open source.
  • Audio transcription uses Whisper and Parakeet models.
  • Summaries come from Qwen and Gemma models running locally.
  • Running locally keeps meeting data on your own machine.
  • The creator plans to try integrating it with Grok Bot.
