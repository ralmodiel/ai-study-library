Grok Bot upgrade: faster agent, X Chat access and new connectors

Summary

The creator lists what's new in Grok Bot. It does computer tasks 3x faster, replies in less than half the time, and has faster voice calls with better memory. It can now read your X Chat messages, you can tag @bot on X to get things done, and email automations are smarter. There are new connectors for Robinhood, Coinbase, Trello, PostHog, beehiiv and more, plus a cleaner UI and better notifications.

Key points

  • Computer tasks are 3x faster and replies take less than half the time
  • Voice calls are faster and have better memory
  • Grok Bot can now access your X Chat messages
  • You can tag @bot on X to have it do tasks
  • Email automations are smarter
  • New connectors: Robinhood, Coinbase, Trello, PostHog, beehiiv and more
  • Chat cards have a cleaner design and notifications are better
