Tip: have your assistant list its routines to cut usage

Summary

A short usage tip: ask your Grok Bot assistant to list all the routines it runs, so you can stop the ones you don't need. This lowers usage and helps you stay within plan limits.

Key points

  • Ask the assistant to list all of its scheduled routines
  • Review the list and stop the routines you no longer need
  • Fewer background routines means less usage and less pressure on rate or plan limits
