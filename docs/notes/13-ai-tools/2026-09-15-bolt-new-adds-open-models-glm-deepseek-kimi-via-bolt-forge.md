## Summary

A short X post by Melvin Vivas sharing bolt.new's announcement of Bolt Forge. Bolt Forge adds the open models GLM, DeepSeek and Kimi to the model picker of bolt.new, an AI app builder. The announcement says the models are free until October 14 with up to 50x more usage and no usage charges. The video has no spoken content, so the only lesson is that open models now compete inside mainstream AI coding tools.

## Key points

- Bolt Forge is a new bolt.new offering that adds open models to its model picker.
- The models offered are GLM, DeepSeek and Kimi, which bolt.new calls 'the new frontier'.
- The announcement says it is free until October 14 with zero usage charges.
- It also claims up to 50x more usage than before.
- It is available now in the model picker on bolt.new.
- Takeaway: open-weight models are becoming a cheap, high-volume option inside AI app-building and coding tools.
