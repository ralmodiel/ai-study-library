Summary

Shares a Grok Bot template that turns the assistant into a scheduled language coach. You pick a language and your level, and it gives you daily lessons, voice drills, quizzes and an 8-week plan with progress tracking. It shows how to build a recurring, scheduled AI assistant workflow.

Key points

  • Choose any language and your proficiency level.
  • Morning: a short lesson.
  • Midday: a voice drill to practise pronunciation.
  • Evening: a review quiz, plus a longer quiz every Sunday.
  • An 8-week plan with a progress tracker.
  • You can also call it anytime for on-demand practice.
