Summary

Melvin Vivas shows that the Cursor iOS app includes a video recording and screenshots of the changes the agent made. This lets you check the agent's work and merge PRs from your phone. The point is that you can review AI-generated changes away from your desk.

Key points

  • The Cursor iOS app shows a video recording of the updates the agent made.
  • It also shows screenshots so you can check UI changes.
  • You can review and merge PRs from your phone without a laptop.
  • Visual evidence (video or screenshots) makes it easier to check agent output before merging.
