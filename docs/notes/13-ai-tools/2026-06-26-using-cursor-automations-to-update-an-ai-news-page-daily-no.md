# Using Cursor Automations to update an AI news page daily, no CMS

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-06-26 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2070428248710975691)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, Portfolio Projects · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

The creator uses Cursor Automations to research fresh AI news every day and update his website's AI news page. He says this saves him a lot of research and editing time, and he no longer uses a CMS. It's an example of an AI coding tool running as a scheduled content agent.

## Key points

- Cursor Automations runs a daily research task that collects fresh AI news.
- The automation updates the site's pages directly, so no CMS is needed.
- The creator says this saves him a lot of manual research and page-editing time.
- The live result is the AI news page on donvitocodes.com.
