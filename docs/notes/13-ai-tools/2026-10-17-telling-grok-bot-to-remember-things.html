Summary

A quick tip that you can tell xAI's Grok bot to remember things, so you can store preferences or facts across conversations simply by asking.

Key points

  • Grok Bot can store information if you explicitly tell it to remember.
  • Explicit memory instructions are a simple way to personalize an assistant over time.
