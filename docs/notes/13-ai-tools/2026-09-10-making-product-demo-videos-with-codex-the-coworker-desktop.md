## Summary

Melvin Vivas shares a 30-second demo video he made with OpenAI's Codex. It shows Coworker, his local-first desktop app of AI coworkers. In the demo, a user picks an AI coworker from a dashboard, asks it for an invoice, and gets back a finished PDF to review. The post mainly shows a coding agent producing a narrated demo video. He notes one flaw: the voiceover mispronounces "PDF" at the end.

## Key points

- The creator used Codex, a coding agent, to produce a narrated product demo video and calls the process 'addicting'.
- The result was nearly usable as-is. The only flaw he mentions is the narrator mispronouncing 'PDF' at the end, so check the generated voiceover for problems with acronyms and terms.
- Coworker is pitched as 'Local-First AI Coworkers for Desktop': AI agents that run on your own machine.
- Demo flow: a dashboard brings everything together, you choose a coworker, give it a task (for example 'need an invoice'), add details in a conversation, and get a finished PDF ready to review.
- The product's main idea is turning a chat conversation into a finished document, with 'less repeating, more doing'.
