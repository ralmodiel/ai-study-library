Summary

Melvin Vivas says he asked Claude Opus 5.5 to turn his landscape livestream teaser into a vertical video, and claims that video editors are no longer needed. The 52-second clip is a montage of short soundbites from the livestream. It mentions running models locally without internet or a GPU, agent loops, running tasks in parallel, a model he trained on his own tweets, and 'inference engineering'. There is very little actual teaching. The post mostly shows an AI model doing a video-editing task and promotes the livestream.

Key points

  • The creator says Claude Opus 5.5 produced a vertical (portrait) version of a landscape livestream teaser, which suggests AI models can take on simple video-editing work.
  • Themes from the livestream: running AI locally ('you don't need internet', 'you don't even need to buy a GPU') and running tasks in parallel.
  • 'Loops are really a way to go' points to agent loops as a way of working.
  • The creator mentions loading a model he trained on his own tweets, an example of personal fine-tuning.
  • He shows a website that looks like a Windows XP machine and jokes that you shouldn't let Codex design your UI.
  • 'Inference engineering' is named as a field of work.
  • The clip has no step-by-step instructions. It is a teaser, and the details are in the full livestream.
