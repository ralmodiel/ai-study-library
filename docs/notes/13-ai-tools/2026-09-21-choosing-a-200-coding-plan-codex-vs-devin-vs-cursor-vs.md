Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-21 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2101870470757032207)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator answers a poll on which $200 coding-assistant plan to buy. He picks Codex Pro 20x, even though its usage limits only last him about 2 days. The post is a quick comparison of the main paid AI coding plans.

## Key points

- Options compared at about $200/month: Codex, Devin, Cursor and Claude.
- The creator's pick is Codex Pro 20x.
- Even on the 20x plan, his heavy use runs out the limits in about 2 days.
