Summary

A short tip: in ChatGPT, set GPT 5.5 to the high reasoning effort setting. The creator says it gives much better ideas.

Key points

  • ChatGPT lets you choose a reasoning effort level for GPT 5.5.
  • According to the creator, the 'high' setting gives clearly better ideas.
  • Higher reasoning effort usually means slower answers, but better results for brainstorming and harder tasks.
