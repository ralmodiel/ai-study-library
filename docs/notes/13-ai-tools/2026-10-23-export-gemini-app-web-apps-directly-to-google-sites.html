Export Gemini App Web Apps Directly to Google Sites

Summary

A quoted announcement says interactive web apps built in the Gemini App can now be exported straight into Google Sites with no code copying. You export to a private site draft, customize it, then publish and share it. The creator adds a short comment on how Gemini's design looks.

Key points

  • Web apps built in the Gemini App can be exported to Google Sites.
  • You don't need to copy code or rebuild the app.
  • The export creates a private site draft that you can customize before publishing.
  • You can share the published site with your team, customers or students.
