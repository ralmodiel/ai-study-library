Coworker: free open-source desktop AI coworker app

Summary

The creator introduces Coworker, a free, open-source desktop app he built. He describes it as a simpler version of Grok Bot and Hermes that works with both local and cloud models, and says it installs easily on Mac, Windows and Linux.

Key points

  • Coworker is a local-first AI coworker desktop app built by the creator.
  • It supports both local and cloud models.
  • It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.
  • It is free and open-source.
  • He positions it as a simpler alternative to Grok Bot and Hermes.
