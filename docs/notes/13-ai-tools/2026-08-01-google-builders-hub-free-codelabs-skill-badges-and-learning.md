## Summary

Google launched Builders Hub, part of the Google Developer Program. The creator says it offers personalized learning, project tracking, 700+ codelabs, skill badges, community access and tools. It's a free place to pick up hands-on skills with Google's AI and developer tools.

## Key points

- Builders Hub sits inside the Google Developer Program (developers.google.com/program)
- Offers personalized learning paths and project tracking
- 700+ hands-on codelabs
- Skill badges you can earn and show on a portfolio
- Includes community access and developer tools
