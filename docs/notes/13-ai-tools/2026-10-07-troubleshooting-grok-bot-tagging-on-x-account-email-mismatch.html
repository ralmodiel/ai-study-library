Troubleshooting Grok Bot Tagging on X: Account Email Mismatch

Summary

Tagging Grok Bot on X did not work for the creator. He suspects the cause is that his Grok Bot uses his Cursor account while his X account uses a different email.

Key points

  • You can call Grok Bot by tagging it on X.
  • Tagging may fail if your Grok Bot account and X account use different emails, e.g. a bot set up through a Cursor account.
  • Use the same identity for the bot and X when troubleshooting.
