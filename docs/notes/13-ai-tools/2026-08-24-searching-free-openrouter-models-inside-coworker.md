# Searching Free OpenRouter Models Inside Coworker

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-24 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2091739444915388799)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator added a search for free models to his Coworker desktop app. You can pick a free OpenRouter model for your agent without visiting the OpenRouter website.

## Key points

- Coworker can search for free models directly in the app.
- You don't need to browse the OpenRouter site to find them.
- Pick a free model for your Coworker agent and you can use AI at no cost.
