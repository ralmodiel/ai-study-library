llama.app: Run Local Models on a Mac Without the llama.cpp CLI

Summary

The llama.cpp team at Hugging Face has launched llama.app, a Mac desktop app for running local LLMs. It picks models that fit your Mac's hardware and is easier to install than using llama.cpp from the command line.

Key points

  • llama.app is a Mac app from the llama.cpp team at Hugging Face.
  • It runs local LLMs that fit your Mac's hardware.
  • It's easier than installing and running llama.cpp from the command line.
  • Good for trying local inference without the cloud or command-line setup.
