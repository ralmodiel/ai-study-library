Using Codex (GPT 5.6 Luna High) for Dataset and Evals Work

Summary

Melvin Vivas finds that Codex with GPT 5.6 Luna on high reasoning effort is good enough to help with dataset and evals work, which is mostly simple tasks. The 5-hour usage limit goes a long way: after 2 hours of use he was at 94%, with 2h 45m left before the reset.

Key points

  • For simple dataset and evals tasks, a mid-tier setup (Codex with GPT 5.6 Luna, high effort) is enough.
  • Codex resets usage every 5 hours; 2 hours of work used 94% of the window.
  • Use cheaper models for routine evals chores.
