Summary

If grok.com shows 'Bots aren't available for this account', the Grok Bot feature isn't enabled for you. In that case, tagging @bot on X to send tasks to Grok Bot won't work. The creator is waiting for his own account to be fixed.

Key points

  • Check grok.com: the message 'Bots aren't available for this account' means Grok Bot is not enabled for you.
  • Without access, tagging @bot on X to send tasks won't work.
  • Access appears to be rolling out gradually; the only option seems to be waiting.
