Summary

Melvin Vivas shares his own 'X Post Tracker' bot, built on Grok Bot. It asks setup questions when you install it, sends a daily digest of how your posts did, alerts you when a post starts taking off, and watches viral posts closely, tracking views per hour and milestones. It's a practical example of an automated agent that monitors things for you.

Key points

  • The bot asks setup questions when you install it, so it's configured for your account
  • It sends a daily digest of how your posts performed
  • It alerts you when a post starts gaining traction
  • It watches viral posts closely, tracking views per hour and milestones
  • It's shared as a downloadable Grok Bot through an x.ai/bot link. That link looked broken when checked.
