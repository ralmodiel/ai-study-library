## Summary

The creator reshares a demo in which GPT-6 Astra built a working iOS app in 13 minutes 3 seconds from a single prompt. The app shows weekly running mileage from Apple Health data. The original poster used their own 'AppCreator' skill and said no other model had made it work from one prompt.

## Key points

- GPT-6 Astra built a working iOS app in 13m 3s from a single prompt.
- The prompt used a custom 'AppCreator' skill.
- Use case: show weekly mileage instead of the averages the Apple Health app gives you.
- The original poster says no other model got it working from one prompt.
