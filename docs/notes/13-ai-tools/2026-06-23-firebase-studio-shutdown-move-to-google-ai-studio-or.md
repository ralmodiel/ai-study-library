Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-06-23 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2069099874478657679)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Firebase Studio is being shut down, and existing projects are asked to migrate to Google AI Studio or Antigravity. Anyone with Firebase Studio projects should plan to move them.

## Key points

- Firebase Studio is being discontinued.
- Existing projects are prompted to migrate to Google AI Studio or Antigravity.
- Pick the migration target that fits your workflow: AI Studio for prompt and app prototyping, Antigravity for IDE-style agentic coding.
