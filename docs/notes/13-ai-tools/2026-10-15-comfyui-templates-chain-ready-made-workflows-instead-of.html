ComfyUI Templates: Chain Ready-Made Workflows Instead of Building From Scratch

This note summarises a video post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 15 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

Melvin Vivas says he uses ComfyUI on Windows to generate AI images and videos locally for free. He recommends learning it if you have a GPU, and notes that templates are a good starting point and coding agents are getting better at editing workflows. The video he quotes is a ComfyUI templates tutorial by Doug. It shows how to join five ready-made templates into one pipeline: a Nano Banana sketch-to-image step, turnaround views, a Hunyuan 3D model, toy-box packaging and a multi-shot video. Claude rewrites the prompts at each step, and no workflow is built from scratch.

Key points

  • You don't need to build a ComfyUI workflow from scratch. Comfy.org has 620+ tested, free workflows, and there's a template browser in the ComfyUI sidebar.
  • Use templates as building blocks, not demos. Copy blocks (node groups) out of different templates and wire them into one canvas.
  • Block 1: a quick drawing plus a creative prompt from Claude goes through Nano Banana 2 to make a stylized character image.
  • Block 2: a turnaround-views template turns the character into multiple views.
  • Block 3: the Hunyuan 3D image-to-model template (from the 3D section of the templates) sends image data into the image-to-3D partner node and returns a mesh, with no Blender or Maya needed.
  • Block 4: the magazine and cover packaging template, with a Nano Banana Pro node and a Claude-refined prompt, generates the toy-box packaging art.
  • Block 5: a video model (said as 'Flux 3 Video') turns one image into a multi-shot video spot, again with a prompt adjusted by Claude.
  • How to learn: take a piece that already works, swap the model, adjust the prompt with Claude, and connect pieces into a new workflow. Running locally needs a GPU with enough VRAM.

Try this

  • If you have a GPU, install ComfyUI and learn it.
  • Browse the 620+ workflows on comfy.org and the template browser in the ComfyUI sidebar.
  • Copy blocks out of several templates and wire them together into one workflow.
  • Swap models and use Claude to adjust each template's prompt.
  • Try coding agents for editing ComfyUI workflows.
  • Make a toy-character pipeline: sketch to Nano Banana image, then turnaround views, then a Hunyuan 3D mesh, then packaging art, then a multi-shot promo video, all in ComfyUI from templates.
  • Remix templates built for something else into a new workflow by swapping models and prompts.
