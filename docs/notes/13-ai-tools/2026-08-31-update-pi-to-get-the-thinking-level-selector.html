Update Pi to Get the Thinking Level Selector

Topics: AI Dev Tools & Productivity · Level: beginner

Summary

The creator tells Pi users to update so they get the new thinking level selector. You open it with /thinking and can save a default or change the level during a session.

Key points

  • Update Pi to get the thinking level selector.
  • Run /thinking and pick a level; press Ctrl+S to make it your startup default.
  • Press Shift+Tab to cycle through levels during a session.
