# Use Your ChatGPT Plus/Pro Subscription to Run Devin

Melvin Vivas · X video post · 2026-09-30 · 0:27 · 690 views · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2105133960296898826)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

This is a short product-news post with no speech. It shares Devin's announcement that you can now sign in to Devin, the AI software-engineering agent, with a ChatGPT Plus or Pro account. When you do, all OpenAI model usage in Devin is taken from your ChatGPT quota. The creator presents this as a way to try Devin "for free," meaning you pay nothing extra if you already have one of those plans.

## Key points

- You can sign in to Devin with a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan.
- Once you're signed in, all OpenAI model usage in Devin comes out of your ChatGPT subscription quota instead of being billed separately.
- This works in all three versions of Devin: Devin Cloud, Devin Desktop and Devin CLI.
- "Free" means no extra cost for people who already pay for ChatGPT Plus or Pro. A paid ChatGPT plan is still required.
- Usage only applies to OpenAI models inside Devin, and it counts against your ChatGPT limits.
