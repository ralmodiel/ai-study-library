Try Devin by Connecting Your ChatGPT Go, Plus or Pro Plan

Summary

A short tip post with no spoken content. It says you can connect a personal ChatGPT plan (Go, Plus or Pro) to Devin, the AI coding agent, and recommends this as the best way to try Devin right now. Once connected, all GPT model usage in Devin draws down from your ChatGPT plan's quota.

Key points

  • Devin now accepts any personal ChatGPT plan as a source of model usage.
  • Supported plans named in the post: ChatGPT Go, Plus and Pro.
  • After you connect a plan, all GPT model usage inside Devin counts against that ChatGPT plan's quota.
  • The creator calls this the best way to try Devin right now, because you reuse a subscription you already pay for.
  • This only covers GPT models. Usage of any other models in Devin isn't covered by the post.
  • The video is 8 seconds long with no speech, so the caption is the whole lesson.
