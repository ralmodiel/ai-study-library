Summary

Announces that Google AI subscription plans now include Google Colab compute credits. This matters to learners who need GPU notebooks for ML and LLM experiments.

Key points

  • Google AI plans now bundle Google Colab credits.
  • Colab credits pay for GPU/TPU notebook compute, which helps with ML training and fine-tuning experiments.
  • If you already pay for a Google AI plan, check whether you can use the included Colab credits.
