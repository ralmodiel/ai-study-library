## Summary

Melvin Vivas shows a short demo of a stop-motion video he made with an AI coding agent. He used Codex with "Astra" to generate scenes with GPT Image 2.5, then had them stitched into a stop-motion video, and says he did no manual edits. The 31-second clip has no narration, so it shows the idea but doesn't explain how to do it step by step.

## Key points

- The workflow: inside Codex, use Astra to generate a series of scenes with the GPT Image 2.5 image model.
- The agent then stitches the generated frames into a stop-motion video.
- The creator says the result needed no manual edits. The agent handled both generating the images and putting the video together.
- This shows a coding agent working as a media-pipeline orchestrator, not just writing code.
- The post doesn't explain what Astra is or give any prompts or settings. Look these up yourself before trying to repeat it.
