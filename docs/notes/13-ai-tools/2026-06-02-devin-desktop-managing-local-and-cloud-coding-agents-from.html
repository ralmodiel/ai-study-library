Summary

Melvin Vivas shares the launch announcement for Devin Desktop and calls it a "must-try." Devin Desktop is a new app from the makers of the Devin AI coding agent. Per the quoted announcement, it lets you manage fleets of local and cloud agents in one place, so you can plan, delegate, review and ship work without leaving your editor. The video itself is a short product ad with no tutorial or technical detail.

Key points

  • Devin Desktop is a new app for managing many coding agents, both local and cloud-based, from a single interface.
  • The workflow it promotes has four steps: plan, delegate to agents, review their output, and ship, all without leaving your editor.
  • The video's message is that every engineer now has an AI 'team' that handles coding, planning, edge cases, testing and incident response.
  • The creator recommends trying it ('Must-try!') but doesn't show a demo, pricing or setup steps.
