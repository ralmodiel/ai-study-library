## Summary

Melvin recommends trying OpenCode, an open-source coding agent. Qwen3.6-Plus and Qwen3.5-Plus are now built into OpenCode Go. He says Qwen models are good for coding.

## Key points

- OpenCode is a coding agent that runs in the terminal and supports many model providers.
- Qwen3.6-Plus and Qwen3.5-Plus are now live in OpenCode Go.
- The creator thinks Qwen models are strong for coding.
- Update OpenCode to get the new Qwen models.
