Model Choice vs. Usage Limits: Astra Burns Codex Pro Quota Fast

Summary

After subscribing to Codex Pro, the creator agrees with other users that the Astra model uses up usage limits quickly, even on the Pro plan. This raises the question of who such an expensive model is really for.

Key points

  • Expensive frontier models can use up a subscription's limits quickly, even on Pro plans.
  • Weigh a model's capability against how fast it uses your quota before making it your default.
  • Consider saving the most expensive model for specific roles, such as orchestration or review, rather than every task.
