Summary

A 20-second clip showing how xAI's Grok voice mode (the creator calls it "Grok Voice 3.0" / "GrokBot") can act as a conversational language tutor. Instead of tapping through app lessons, the creator tells Grok he is a beginner and practices speaking Japanese with it live from his phone. He argues this could replace apps like Duolingo. It is a quick product demo and opinion, not a technical lesson.

Key points

  • The creator says the new Grok Voice 3.0 lets you use Grok ("GrokBot") as a personal language tutor.
  • The method: tell Grok you're a beginner, then just start a spoken conversation in the target language (Japanese in the demo).
  • You practice speaking in a real conversation instead of tapping through set lessons.
  • It runs from the phone, so you can practice anytime.
  • The creator's take: "Duolingo is dead." This is his opinion, framed as a question about the future of language learning.
  • This is an example of voice-first AI products competing with traditional learning apps.
