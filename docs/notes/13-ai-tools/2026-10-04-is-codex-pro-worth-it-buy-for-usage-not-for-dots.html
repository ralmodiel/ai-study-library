Is Codex Pro Worth It? Buy for Usage, Not for Dots

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 4 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

A short opinion on OpenAI's Codex Pro subscription. The creator says the Dots feature isn't a reason to upgrade. Extra usage is, and GPT-6.1 Sol goes a long way even on the $100 plan.

Key points

  • Don't upgrade to Codex Pro just to get the Dots feature
  • Upgrading makes sense if you need more usage
  • GPT-6.1 Sol goes a long way even on the $100 plan
