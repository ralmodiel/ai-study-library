## Summary

Melvin Vivas reposts a CapCut post showing "Doll Dreamer," a 3:45 puppet-style short film made with ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 video model inside CapCut Video Studio. It is a demo, not a tutorial. The video tells the story of Charlie, a doll who can't leave his town, and shows that current AI video tools can produce a consistent character, several scenes, narration and dialogue in one film. It doesn't explain any workflow, prompts or settings.

## Key points

- Seedance 2.0 (ByteDance's AI video model) was used to make a puppet/doll-style animated short film.
- The film was made in CapCut Video Studio by the invited creator @twice_adad2 for CapCut's promotional post.
- The film is about 3:45 long. It keeps the same main character (Charlie) across many scenes: a gas station, a harbor, the sea and a storm.
- It combines voice-over narration with character dialogue, which suggests AI video and audio tools can now handle a story with several scenes.
- No prompts, workflow steps, costs or settings are shared, so treat it as a look at what the tool can do, not as a how-to.
