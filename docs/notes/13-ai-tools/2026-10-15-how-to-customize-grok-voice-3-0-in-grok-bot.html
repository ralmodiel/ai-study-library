Summary

Step-by-step instructions for setting up Grok Voice 3.0 inside the Grok Bot assistant: open voice mode, then change speaking speed, language, voice and personality in Settings.

Key points

  • Open Voice Mode with the voice icon in Grok Bot.
  • In Settings, set speaking speed (up to 2x) and language.
  • Pick your preferred voice and personality.
  • You can leave language detection on Auto.
