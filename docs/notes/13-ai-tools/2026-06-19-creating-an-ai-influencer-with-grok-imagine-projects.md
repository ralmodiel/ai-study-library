## Summary

The creator shows how to create an AI influencer with Grok Imagine. Its Projects feature keeps AI-generated images and videos together in one project.

## Key points

- Grok Imagine can generate both images and videos.
- Projects keep related AI-generated media together, which makes it easier to build a consistent character such as an AI influencer.
