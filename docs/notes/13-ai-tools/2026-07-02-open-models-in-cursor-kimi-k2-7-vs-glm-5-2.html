Summary

Cursor now supports open-weight models, and Kimi K2.7 is available inside the editor. The quoted post shares Cursor's own eval results comparing Kimi K2.7 with GLM 5.2. This means you can choose cheaper open models next to closed models in the same coding assistant.

Key points

  • Cursor now supports open models alongside closed ones.
  • Kimi K2.7 (by Moonshot AI) can be selected as a model in Cursor.
  • Cursor published eval results comparing Kimi K2.7 with GLM 5.2.
  • Open models can be a cheaper option for agentic coding work.
