Luna, Not Sol, as the Daily Driver on Codex Plus

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 5 September 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

The creator replies to advice that Plus users should use Sol daily and Astra only for complex tasks. He says even Sol uses too much quota on Plus, and trying Astra was painful. Luna is the practical daily model on the Plus plan.

Key points

  • The quoted advice: use Astra only for complex tasks and Sol as the daily driver.
  • The creator's view: Sol also uses up Plus limits too fast to be a daily driver.
  • Astra is available on Plus but drains usage very quickly.
  • Use Luna as the daily driver on the Plus plan.
