## Summary

This is a short product-news post. Melvin Vivas shares Anthropic's announcement that Claude Cowork, its agent for delegating tasks, is coming to mobile and web. You can start a task on your computer, close the laptop while Claude keeps working, and pick up the finished result on your phone. The beta starts with the Max plan and will reach more plans over the coming weeks.

## Key points

- Claude Cowork is expanding from desktop to mobile and web.
- The workflow: give Claude a task at your desk, then collect the finished work later from your phone.
- Tasks keep running after you close your laptop, so the agent works in the background without you.
- The beta rolls out over several weeks, starting with the Claude Max plan, and more plans will follow.
- This is part of a wider trend of AI agents that run long tasks in the background and work across devices.
- The video has no narration, so this post contains no technical walkthrough.
