# Watching Codex weekly limits: GPT 5.6 Sol vs Luna max cost

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-17 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2089258386143769077)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, LLMOps, Deployment & Monitoring · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator watched his Codex weekly usage limit drop quickly during normal prompting on a small codebase, and blames the expensive GPT 5.6 Sol model. He plans to switch to the Luna max model to make his limits last longer. The lesson: which model you pick strongly affects how fast you use up your plan.

## Key points

- Codex shows your weekly usage limit, and you can watch it go down as you prompt.
- GPT 5.6 Sol (the top model) uses up limits fast, even on a small codebase.
- Switching to a cheaper model (Luna max) makes your weekly limit last longer.
- The best model isn't always needed. Match the model to the task to control cost.
