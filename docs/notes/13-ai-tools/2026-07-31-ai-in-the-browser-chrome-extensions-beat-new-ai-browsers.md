# AI in the Browser: Chrome Extensions Beat New AI Browsers

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-07-31 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2083111378987225372)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The creator argues that a Chrome extension is a better way to get AI into your browser than switching to a new AI browser, and says this is why ChatGPT Atlas and Comet didn't catch on. He uses the Claude and ChatGPT Chrome extensions to automate his web research and says both work well. He plans to try the Hermes extension next.

## Key points

- Opinion: AI added to the browser you already use (an extension) is better UX than a separate AI browser
- The creator says AI browsers ChatGPT Atlas and Comet failed to catch on
- Claude and ChatGPT Chrome extensions work well for automating web research
- A Hermes browser extension also exists; the creator hasn't tested it yet
