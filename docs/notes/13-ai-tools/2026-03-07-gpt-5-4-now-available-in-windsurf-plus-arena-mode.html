Summary

News post: Windsurf added OpenAI's GPT-5.4 with several reasoning-effort levels. GPT-5.4 Low can also be used in Windsurf's Arena Mode (Frontier and Hybrid Battle Groups), where models are compared head to head. It shows that coding IDEs now offer new frontier models with a choice of reasoning effort.

Key points

  • Windsurf offers GPT-5.4 at several reasoning-effort levels.
  • GPT-5.4 Low is available in Arena Mode's Frontier and Hybrid Battle Groups.
  • Arena Mode lets you compare models side by side on your own coding tasks.
  • Choosing a reasoning effort means trading speed and cost against answer quality.
