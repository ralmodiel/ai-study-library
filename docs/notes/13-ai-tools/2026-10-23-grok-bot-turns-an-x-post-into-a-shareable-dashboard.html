This is an 18-second demo. Melvin Vivas asks Grok Bot to turn one of his X posts into a dashboard, and it builds a clean visual one. The dashboard can be shared with teammates by copying a link, and Grok can keep it up to date. It's a quick look at a product feature, not a technical lesson.
