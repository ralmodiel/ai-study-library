Summary

GPT-5.6 Sol was 50% off on OpenRouter when this was posted. The creator suggests using it through OpenRouter with Codex once your Codex plan limits are used up, as a cheaper option than upgrading your plan.

Key points

  • GPT-5.6 Sol is listed on OpenRouter at a 50% discount (time-limited).
  • OpenRouter gives API access to many models, billed per use.
  • You can point Codex at OpenRouter as a fallback when your subscription limits are used up.
  • Pay-per-use API access can be cheaper than moving to a higher plan tier.
