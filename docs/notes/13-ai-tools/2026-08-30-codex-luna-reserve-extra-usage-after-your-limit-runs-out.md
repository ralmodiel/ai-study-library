# Codex Luna reserve: extra usage after your limit runs out

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-30 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2093734325753958654)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The Luna reserve works in the Codex app and runs on GPT 5.6 Luna light. It gives you some extra usage after your main limit runs out, so you don't have to wait for the 5-hour limit to reset.

## Key points

- The Luna reserve works in the Codex app.
- The reserve runs on the GPT 5.6 Luna light model.
- It gives you extra usage after your normal limit is used up.
- You can keep working instead of waiting for the 5-hour limit to reset.
- Expect a lighter model while on the reserve; the creator found GPT 5.6 Luna weak at planning in a separate post.
