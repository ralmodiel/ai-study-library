Launch an AI Voice Assistant (Grok Bot) from the iPhone Action Button

Summary

Melvin Vivas shows that he mapped his iPhone's Action Button to open "Grok Bot" voice chat, so a voice AI assistant starts with one press. The 39-second demo is only a short voice chat with the assistant, which he calls "Emma". It mentions "open threads" and says Gmail forwarding is now live, which suggests a personal assistant connected to his tools. The post promises setup steps, but neither the caption nor the transcript actually walks through them.

Key points

  • The iPhone Action Button (on supported models) can be set to open an app or a Shortcut. Here it opens Grok Bot voice chat in one press.
  • With this shortcut you can start a hands-free voice session with your AI assistant right away, without unlocking the phone and finding the app.
  • In the demo the assistant ("Emma") greets him by name and talks about ongoing work, including "open threads" and Gmail forwarding being live. That points to a personal assistant linked to email and tasks.
  • The setup steps aren't in the material. The usual path is Settings > Action Button, then choose an app or a Shortcut that opens the assistant's voice mode.
  • It's unclear whether "Grok Bot" is xAI's Grok app or a custom bot built on Grok. Check this before you try to copy the setup.
