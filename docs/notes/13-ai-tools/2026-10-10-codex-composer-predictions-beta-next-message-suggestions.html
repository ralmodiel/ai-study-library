Summary

A short reaction post to OpenAI's announcement that "composer predictions" is in beta in Codex for Pro users. The feature suggests your next message based on the conversation and how you usually talk to Codex. The creator guesses it isn't offered on the Plus plan because it uses a lot of tokens. The video has no spoken content to learn from.

Key points

  • OpenAI's Codex has a new beta feature called composer predictions.
  • Composer predictions suggests your next message based on the current conversation and your usual way of prompting Codex.
  • For now it is only for Pro subscribers, not Plus.
  • OpenAI says it is one of the most loved features it has ever tested internally.
  • The creator guesses it is limited to Pro because generating predictions uses a lot of tokens. This is his guess, not an official reason.
  • The 15-second video only contains the words "We'll be right back" and adds nothing.
