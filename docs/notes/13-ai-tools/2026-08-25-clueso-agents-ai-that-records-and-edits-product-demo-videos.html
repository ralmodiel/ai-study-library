Clueso Agents: AI That Records and Edits Product Demo Videos for You

Summary

Melvin Vivas reshares Clueso's launch video. The video says Clueso agents can make a full product screen-recording from one prompt. The agent opens the right screens, clicks through the flow at a human pace, records it, and then edits it with zoom-ins, spotlights, captions, AI voiceovers and on-brand animations. It is mostly a product promotion, but it shows a real use for agents that control a computer.

Key points

  • Manual screen recording takes a lot of work: setting up screens, writing a script, rehearsing cursor movements and narrating in sync. One mistake often means starting over.
  • With Clueso agents, you describe in a prompt what you want recorded, and the agent makes the video.
  • The agent opens the right screens in your product and goes through the flow by itself at a clean, human pace while recording.
  • In editing, it adds zoom-in effects timed to the action and spotlights that point attention to important UI elements.
  • In the final polish, it adds captions, AI voiceovers and animations, and keeps everything on-brand.
  • Clueso presents this as the difference from tools like Loom and Screen Studio, where you still do the recording yourself.
  • Broader lesson: agents that use a browser or computer can automate repetitive content work, such as demos and tutorials, from start to finish.
