## Summary

Bashiri Smith shares five patterns he saw over two years of helping engineers land jobs worth more than $3M in combined salary. The five are: have solid engineering fundamentals, stay consistent well past six months, build projects worth showing, be easy to work with, and have a real reason for doing this beyond money. He says today's job market is more competitive than in 2020, so you need an edge. He ends by pitching his community.

## Key points

- Don't suck: you need at least intermediate software engineering skills to land technical roles (forward deployed, AI solutions, AI engineering, software engineering).
- Be consistent: most people try for 2-3 months and then quit or stay comfortable. Plan to keep going for well over 6 months.
- Build: engineers who build in their own time stand out from about 90% of other engineers. Without projects worth looking at, interviews won't come.
- Be sociable: you don't have to be an extrovert, but being easy to talk to and work with brings more opportunities.
- Care: the engineers who get hired fastest usually aren't just chasing money. Know your own reason for being in tech.
- The market has changed since 2020: knowing how to code no longer gets you a job right away. It's competitive now and you need an edge.
