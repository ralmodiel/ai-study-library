# Pure dev roles are fading: product knowledge is now essential

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-06-29 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2071594206699688061)

**Topics:** Resume, Job Search & Interviews, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Melvin argues that technical roles have changed: engineers can no longer focus only on development. Product knowledge is becoming an essential skill alongside engineering.

## Key points

- Developer roles are becoming broader than pure coding.
- Product knowledge (understanding users, features and business value) is becoming a core skill.
- Build product sense alongside your technical skills to stay competitive.
