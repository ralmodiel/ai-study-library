3 Worst Mistakes When Trying to Land an AI Engineer Job

Summary

Bashiri Smith lists three mistakes that hold back aspiring AI engineers. The first is skipping software engineering fundamentals. The second is spending too long on tutorials when you should be building projects. The third is waiting too long to apply for jobs. He says to learn the core AI engineering topics, build 2–3 good projects, put key AI terms on your resume and apply right away.

Key points

  • Mistake 1: skipping software engineering fundamentals. AI engineering grows out of software engineering, so without the basics you're 'pretty cooked'.
  • Mistake 2: spending too long on tutorials. AI changes every week, so you could keep learning for years without ever being done.
  • Learn the basics of RAG, agents, ops, evaluation, safety and machine learning, then learn by building projects.
  • Mistake 3: waiting until you feel ready before applying for jobs.
  • If you have 2–3 good projects, an internship or past engineering experience, start applying now.
  • Put key AI terms on your resume and let the job market tell you whether you're ready.

Try this

  • Build solid software engineering fundamentals before focusing on AI.
  • Learn the basics of RAG, agents, ops, evaluation, safety and machine learning, then stop doing tutorials.
  • Learn by building projects instead of watching more tutorials.
  • Get 2–3 good projects on your resume.
  • Add key AI terms to your resume.
  • Start applying for jobs right away and let the market judge whether you're ready.
