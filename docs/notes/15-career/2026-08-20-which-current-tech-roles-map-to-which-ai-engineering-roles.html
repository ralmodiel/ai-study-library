Summary

This short reel says people already working in software, data or business analysis are well placed to move into AI engineering. It pairs each of these roles with a matching AI role and claims the switch "nearly guarantees" a 20% raise, but gives no source for that number. Most of the reel promotes the creator's BASWE.Ai Engineer community on Skool.

Key points

  • Software Engineer → Applied AI Engineer.
  • Data Engineer → AI Engineer or AI Platform Engineer.
  • Data Scientist → AI / ML Engineer.
  • Data Analyst and Business Analyst → AI Solutions Engineer.
  • The creator claims moving into AI engineering 'nearly guarantees' a 20% pay increase, and the caption hints at doubling your salary. Neither claim has a source.
  • He says companies are hiring engineers who can actually build and ship AI systems.
  • The hard parts he names are knowing what to learn, what to build, and how to present your existing experience for AI roles.
