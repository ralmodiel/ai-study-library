How to Grow an X (Twitter) Account: 9 Practical Tips from Melvin Vivas

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 26 September 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

Melvin Vivas lost his original X account and rebuilt a new one. Three months later he had a four-digit creator payout for a two-week period. He shares tips for growing an audience on X: get verified, stay in one niche, post original content, use X Pro to follow what's happening, be authentic and don't use AI to write replies, post often, engage in the first hour after posting, and check your analytics every day. It's useful for AI engineers building a public profile, but it doesn't teach AI engineering.

Key points

  • Get verified. People trust a blue checkmark more. He uses Premium+, which also comes with Grok.
  • Stick to one theme or niche. Don't post random things.
  • Post original content. The new creator program penalizes accounts that copy others.
  • Use X Pro (pro.x.com) as a dashboard to keep up with what's happening on X. He stays up late in Singapore to catch US AI announcements.
  • Be authentic and kind. Saying nasty things hurts your account.
  • Never use AI to write replies. People can tell, and he blocks accounts that do it.
  • Post things even if you don't expect them to go viral. You can't predict which posts will take off.
  • Watch each post's views in the first hour and reply to people who engage. Check your analytics every day.

Try this

  • Get verified on X (Premium or Premium+).
  • Pick one niche (for example, AI engineering) and keep your posts on it.
  • Post only original content. Don't copy other people's posts.
  • Set up X Pro to track AI announcements and news in your niche.
  • Write your own replies, without AI, and keep them kind.
  • Post regularly, even when a post doesn't seem viral.
  • For the first hour after posting, reply to everyone who engages.
  • Check X Analytics every day.
