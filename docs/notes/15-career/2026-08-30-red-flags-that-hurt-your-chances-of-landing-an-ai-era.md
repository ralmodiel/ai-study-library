## Summary

A 13-second hook video listing warning signs that a developer won't be competitive for coding and AI engineering jobs in the next few years. These include depending on AI to build basic projects, never having read research on arXiv, never building with modern GenAI stacks like RAG, and having a resume full of coursework instead of projects. It doesn't teach any skills itself. Instead, it points viewers to the creator's free AI Engineer Roadmap video and his paid-style Skool coaching community.

## Key points

- Be able to build a basic project yourself without AI writing everything for you.
- Get familiar with arXiv.org and read AI research papers.
- Build real projects with modern GenAI tech stacks, such as RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation).
- Fill your resume with real projects, not coursework.
- Don't assume AI will take every coding job. The developers who win will be the ones who know how to build with AI.
- Have a concrete career plan, not just hope.
- The creator says AI engineering roles pay $100K-$200K+ and points to his free roadmap training as the next step.
