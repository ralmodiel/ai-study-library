Tips for building an audience on X. The creator says account size no longer matters: new followers come from viral posts. Key points

  • Followers come as a result of exposure from viral posts
  • Follow what's happening on X and post your own take
  • Don't just repeat what others have already said
  • Be patient and keep posting; you'll do better once the algorithm picks you up
  • Use a tracker bot (the creator's X Post Tracker template for Grok Bot) to keep up with trends
