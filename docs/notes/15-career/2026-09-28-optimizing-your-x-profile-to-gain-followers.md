# Optimizing Your X Profile to Gain Followers

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-28 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2104373603571790080)

**Topics:** Resume, Job Search & Interviews · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Personal-branding tips for X (Twitter): the parts of your profile visitors see first and how to set them up to turn visits into follows. This helps AI engineers build a public presence around their work.

## Key points

- Profile pic: keep it clean with a plain background
- Bio: use multiple lines of short phrases
- Cover photo: show what you are about
- Pinned post: pin your current viral post, or a project you're working on so people see what you do
- These elements are what visitors see first, so experiment with them

## Try this

- [ ] Update your profile pic to a clean image with a plain background
- [ ] Rewrite your bio as several short lines
- [ ] Set a cover photo that shows what you do
- [ ] Pin a viral post or a current project
- [ ] Experiment and iterate on your profile
