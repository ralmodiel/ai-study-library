Summary

A quick checklist for software engineers moving into AI engineering. It covers where to find AI jobs, which tech stack to learn (Python, LangChain, PyTorch, FastAPI), five portfolio projects you can explain in an interview, where to learn the skills, and how to keep up with research. The main advice is to pick one project, go deep on it, and be ready to explain your trade-offs between quality, cost, latency and reliability.

Key points

  • Job boards for AI engineering roles: Hiring Cafe, Welcome to the Jungle, Wellfound and YC Jobs.
  • Core tech stack: Python, LangChain, PyTorch and FastAPI.
  • Five suggested projects: Knowledge Graph RAG over enterprise data, a multi-agent research assistant, a self-healing LLM gateway, LLM-as-judge evals calibrated against human scores, and a model distillation pipeline.
  • Places to learn the skills: DeepLearning.AI, Hugging Face and the creator's Skool community (BASWE.Ai Engineer).
  • To stay current, follow engineers and researchers on X and look up the papers they discuss on arXiv.org.
  • Pick one project and go deep: deploy it, measure where it fails, and write down what you changed.
  • Be ready to explain your decisions on quality, cost, latency and reliability. That gives you a concrete answer when a hiring manager asks 'What have you actually built?'
