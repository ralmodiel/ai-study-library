# Find Hidden AI Jobs: Search by Skills (RAG, LLMs), Not "AI Engineer" Titles

Bashiri Smith · Facebook reel · 2026-09-28 · 1:03 · 4,588 views · [Open on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2247063872805427)

**Topics:** Resume, Job Search & Interviews, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Bashiri Smith points to an Andela study of Fortune 500 technical job postings. It found that over half of job titles don't match the actual work, and thousands of postings asking for AI engineering skills weren't titled "AI Engineer." If you only search for "AI Engineer," you miss many roles you qualify for. Instead, search by the skills you actually use (RAG, LLMs, vector databases, embeddings) and judge each role by its responsibilities, not its title.

## Key points

- Andela analyzed 47,101 technical job postings from Fortune 500 companies (the transcript says 'over 45,000') and found that over half of job titles don't match the actual work.
- Almost 7,000 postings asked for AI engineering skills, such as building AI applications, but none were titled 'AI Engineer.'
- Some 'Data Scientist' roles actually ask you to implement RAG and build AI agents.
- Searching only 'AI Engineer' on job boards hides many relevant roles.
- Search for skill keywords instead: RAG, LLM, vector databases, embeddings, plus any other skill you'd use day to day.
- Read the job description and responsibilities before deciding whether a role fits. Don't judge by the title alone.
