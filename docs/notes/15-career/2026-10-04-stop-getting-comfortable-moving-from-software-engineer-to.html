Summary

A motivational reel arguing that comfort in a current software job, not lack of resources, is what keeps engineers from moving into AI engineering. The creator says a working software engineer can make the move in about 3-4 months with focused after-work study and weekend projects, and can get at least a 20% raise. Most of the reel promotes the creator's BASWE.AI Engineer community on Skool.

Key points

  • Common excuses: 'I don't know where to start', 'AI is moving too fast', 'I'm tired after work', 'no time to study'. The creator calls these excuses that come from comfort.
  • If you keep saying you want to earn more or get into AI, ask yourself why you aren't studying after work or building projects on weekends.
  • If you are building projects, treat them seriously and actually apply for AI roles.
  • Creator's estimate: a working software engineer needs at most 3-4 months to move into AI engineering.
  • Claimed result: at least a 20% raise when you switch into AI engineering.
  • Suggested loop: build AI projects, get feedback on them, then apply for the roles you want.

Try this

  • Set aside regular time after work to study AI engineering.
  • Build AI projects on weekends and take them seriously.
  • Get feedback on your AI projects.
  • Apply for AI engineering roles instead of staying in a comfortable job.
