Hiring the hard 'AI-native operator' role: look beyond the US

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 10 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

AI startups are fighting to hire people who follow trends, have creative taste, understand AI technically and can execute well. The creator says this talent exists globally on X, so companies that only hire in the US limit themselves.

Key points

  • A role in high demand at AI startups: someone chronically online who knows trends.
  • They also need taste and the ability to create new media content.
  • They must understand AI and be technical.
  • And they must execute with quality, like an operator.
  • Hiring only in the US makes the role much harder to fill. Many talented people elsewhere on X fit the profile.
  • For job seekers: combining technical AI skills with content creation and trend awareness is a rare, valued mix.

Try this

  • Build visible skills in both AI engineering and content creation to fit this in-demand hybrid role.
