# Using the September Hiring Surge: Volume Plus Quality in Your Job Search

Bashiri Smith · Facebook reel · 2026-09-03 · 0:29 · 18,193 views · [Open on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1819927632698021)

**Topics:** Resume, Job Search & Interviews, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

Bashiri Smith explains that the "September Surge" is the second-biggest hiring season of the year. Companies pick hiring back up after summer, so more applicants are competing too. His advice is to send more applications without letting quality drop: keep tailoring resumes, reach out directly to recruiters and hiring managers, and post your projects publicly. He links a free training video with a full walkthrough.

## Key points

- September is the second-biggest hiring season of the year. Companies ramp hiring back up after the summer slowdown.
- More open jobs also means more applicants, so expect more competition.
- Send more applications, but don't just spam LinkedIn Easy Apply.
- Tailor your resume for the roles that fit you well.
- Send warm outreach to recruiters and hiring managers.
- Post your projects on LinkedIn and X so people can see them.
- Stay as active as you can while companies are hiring. Aim for both volume and quality.
