Open the original · 80 pages

An 80-page interview prep guide from the baswe.Ai Engineer Accelerator. It has 100 AI engineering interview questions in five modules of 20 each: LLMs and RAG, Agents and orchestration, Ops and evaluation, Safety and ethics, and ML foundations. Every question says what the interviewer is testing, outlines a strong answer and gives the follow-up they will likely push on. The guide tells you to practise each answer out loud in under two minutes, always attach a number, name the trade-off, say how you would measure it and raise failure modes before you are asked.

    Open standard for connecting models to tools and data sources through one interface. It turns an N×M integration problem into N+M.

  • Firecracker · tool · firecracker-microvm.github.io · free
    MicroVM the guide names for running model-generated code in isolation, one per run.
  • garak · tool · github.com · free
    Tool the guide names for running an automated prompt-injection and jailbreak attack suite on every release, as part of red-teaming.

    Typed-model library the guide names for validating structured LLM output, retrying once with the validation error if it fails.
