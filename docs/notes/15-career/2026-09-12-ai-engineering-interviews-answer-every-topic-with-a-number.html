Summary

An 8-second reel with no speech. It shows the interview topics the creator says decide AI engineering offers, but the list itself is only on screen and isn't in the caption. The one lesson: you don't need to master every topic, but you should be able to talk about any of them using a concrete number, a trade-off and a failure mode. That is what makes you sound like someone who has shipped, not someone who read a tutorial. The rest of the caption promotes the creator's guide of 100 interview questions and his AI engineering community.

Key points

  • The topic list is only shown on screen in the video. The caption and transcript don't include it, so watch the reel to see the topics.
  • You don't need to know every interview topic perfectly. You do need to be able to speak on any of them.
  • Build each answer from three parts: a concrete number (a metric, latency, cost or scale), a trade-off you weighed, and a failure mode you've seen or planned for.
  • Numbers, trade-offs and failure modes make you sound like you've shipped. Generic explanations make you sound like you only read a tutorial.
  • The creator's guide of 100 AI engineering interview questions is said to cover every topic on the list.
  • The creator's community runs weekly sessions: a resume review with a Spotify recruiter, interview-question breakdowns (including one with a Shopify AI/ML engineer), an interview workshop with a Lockheed Martin software engineer, and a call with a data science and ML executive.
