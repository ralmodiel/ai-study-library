Summary

This short skit says many applicants get few interviews because recruiters don't trust text-only claims on resumes and LinkedIn. The fix it suggests is to record video walkthroughs of your projects and link them from your resume and LinkedIn, so recruiters can see that you built the work and understand it. The reel ends by promoting the creator's BASWE.Ai Engineer community on Skool.

Key points

  • A resume or LinkedIn profile that only lists projects in text gives no proof that you built them.
  • The creator cites an unnamed 2026 hiring report saying over 70% of recruiters don't trust applicants because of fake credentials.
  • Record video walkthroughs of your projects and link them from your resume and LinkedIn.
  • In each walkthrough, cover the problem you're solving, how you found it, the tools you used, and the system design.
  • Post about your projects on LinkedIn often while you keep building them.
  • A clickable video lets recruiters check that you built the project and know what you're talking about.

  • Add the walkthrough links to your resume and LinkedIn profile.
