# Open-weight Nemotron models for finance and healthcare

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-02 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2095009260224876793)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, Fine-tuning & Model Customization, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

Fastino and NVIDIA released two open-weight models for specific industries, built on Nemotron 3.5 Lightning: one for finance and one for healthcare. The creator says they're worth a look if you work in those industries. They're examples of specialized open models you might use instead of a general LLM.

## Key points

- Fastino-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-Finance and Fastino-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-Healthcare are open-weight models.
- Both are built on NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Lightning base model, made together with the Nemotron team.
- Industry-specific open models are an option for finance and healthcare work, where you may need to host the model yourself.
