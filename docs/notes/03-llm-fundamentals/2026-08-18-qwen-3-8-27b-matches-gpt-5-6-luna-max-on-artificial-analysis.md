# Qwen 3.8 27B Matches GPT 5.6 Luna (max) on Artificial Analysis

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-18 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2089423087980957862)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

On Artificial Analysis's intelligence comparison, Qwen 3.8 27B, a fairly small open-weight model, scores about the same as GPT 5.6 Luna at max reasoning. The post shows how to use independent benchmark sites to compare models.

## Key points

- Qwen 3.8 27B scores on par with GPT 5.6 Luna (max) on Artificial Analysis's intelligence comparison.
- A 27B open-weight model now competes with a frontier proprietary model on this index.
- Artificial Analysis compares models on intelligence, performance (speed) and price.
- Check independent leaderboards like this one when choosing a model, rather than relying only on vendor claims.
