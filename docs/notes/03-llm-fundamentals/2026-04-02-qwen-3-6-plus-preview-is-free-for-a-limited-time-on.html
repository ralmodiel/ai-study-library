Summary

Alibaba's Qwen 3.6 Plus Preview is free on OpenRouter for a limited time. During the free period, prompts and completions are collected and may be used to improve the model.

Key points

  • Qwen 3.6 Plus Preview from Alibaba Qwen is free on OpenRouter for a limited time.
  • Privacy caveat: during the free period, your prompts and completions are logged and may be used for training.
  • Do not send sensitive or proprietary data to free preview endpoints.
  • OpenRouter gives you one API for many model providers, so you can try new models with little effort.
