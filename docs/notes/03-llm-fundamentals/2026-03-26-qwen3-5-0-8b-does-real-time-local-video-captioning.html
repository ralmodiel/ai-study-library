Qwen3.5 0.8B Does Real-Time Local Video Captioning

This note summarises a video post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 26 March 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Topics: LLM Fundamentals, Industry Trends & Job Market · Level: intermediate

Summary

A roughly 1GB Qwen3.5 0.8B model captions video in real time on a Mac Studio M2 Ultra. It takes under 1 second per frame and streams scene descriptions as the video plays. This shows how capable tiny local vision-language models have become.

Key points

  • Model: Qwen3.5 0.8B, about 1GB.
  • Hardware: Mac Studio M2 Ultra, running fully locally.
  • Under 1s per frame; 269 frames captioned from a 3m49s video.
  • Descriptions stream as the video plays, and you can pause anywhere to see its understanding of that scene.
