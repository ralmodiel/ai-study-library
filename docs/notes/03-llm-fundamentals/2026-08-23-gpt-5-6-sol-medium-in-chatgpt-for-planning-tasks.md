The creator recommends GPT 5.6 Sol at medium reasoning effort in ChatGPT for planning. He says it balances speed and reasoning well. This is a short tip on picking a model and reasoning level for each task.

- Choose the reasoning level for the task: planning doesn't always need the highest setting.
