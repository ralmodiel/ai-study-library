Summary

OpenRouter's stealth model 'Space Bunny', which stayed #1 for 10 days, turned out to be MiniMax M3.1 Flash. The final version is live on OpenRouter and free for 72 hours. Stealth models on OpenRouter are an early chance to try upcoming models.

Key points

  • 'Space Bunny' was MiniMax M3.1 Flash under a stealth name.
  • It held the #1 spot on OpenRouter for 10 straight days.
  • Developers were using an early build; the final M3.1 Flash is now released.
  • It was free on OpenRouter for 72 hours after launch.
