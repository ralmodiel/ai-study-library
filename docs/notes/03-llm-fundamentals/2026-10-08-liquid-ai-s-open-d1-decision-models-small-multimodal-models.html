Liquid AI's open d1 decision models: small multimodal models that run locally

Summary

This short post from Melvin Vivas points to Liquid AI's newly released open "d1 decision models" and says he is about to try them. The quoted Liquid AI post says the models are small enough to run on a MacBook and are multimodal, so they accept inputs like video. In its demo, d1-3B rates an outfit. The video has no speech, so there is no deeper technical walkthrough.

Key points

  • Liquid AI released d1, a family of open "decision models".
  • The models are small enough to run locally on a laptop such as a MacBook.
  • They are multimodal and can take video as input.
  • The demo uses the d1-3B variant (about 3B parameters) to judge or rate something it sees in a video, in this case an outfit.
  • Liquid AI hosts more demos on its Hugging Face Space.
  • Takeaway: small open multimodal models now make on-device judging and classification of video practical.
