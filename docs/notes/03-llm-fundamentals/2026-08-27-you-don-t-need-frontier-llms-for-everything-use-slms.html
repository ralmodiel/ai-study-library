You don't need frontier LLMs for everything: use SLMs

Summary

The creator recommends a talk by Rachel Nabors arguing that you don't need the biggest, most advanced (frontier) LLMs for every job. For specialized tasks, task-specific models or small language models (SLMs) can be the better choice. The post does not name or link the talk.

Key points

  • Frontier LLMs are not needed for every task.
  • Task-specific models or SLMs can handle specialized tasks.
  • When choosing a model, match its size and abilities to the task, not just the top benchmark scores.
