Summary

This short post shares Mistral AI's announcement of Mistral Large 4, which Mistral nicknames "Le Chonk". Mistral says it is an open-weights, natively multimodal model with 1T total parameters and 49B active per token, which points to a mixture-of-experts design. Mistral calls it the best open-weights model from the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks. The creator says he has never used Mistral and asks what it is good at. The video itself has no real content.

Key points

  • Mistral Large 4 is a new model from Mistral AI, nicknamed "Le Chonk".
  • It has 1 trillion total parameters, but only 49B are active per token. That ratio suggests a sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) design.
  • Mistral says it is natively multimodal and released with open weights.
  • Mistral claims it is the best open-weights model from the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks.
  • Mistral claims state-of-the-art results on high-stakes workloads: cyber defense, manufacturing and finance.
  • Total vs. active parameters matters when choosing a model: active parameters drive the compute cost per token, while total parameters drive how much memory you need to host it.
