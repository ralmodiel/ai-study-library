Summary

NVIDIA released a quantized GLM-5.2 checkpoint on Hugging Face under the MIT License. That makes a frontier-level open model easier to self-host and usable commercially.

Key points

  • NVIDIA published a quantized GLM-5.2 on Hugging Face.
  • It is released under the permissive MIT License.
  • Quantized checkpoints lower the memory needed to self-host large open models.
