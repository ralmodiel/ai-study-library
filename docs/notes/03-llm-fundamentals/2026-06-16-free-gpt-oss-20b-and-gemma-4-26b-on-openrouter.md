# Free gpt-oss-20b and Gemma 4 26B on OpenRouter

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-06-16 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2066607092895809536)

**Topics:** LLM Fundamentals, LLMOps, Deployment & Monitoring · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

OpenRouter added free capacity for two open-weight models, gpt-oss-20b and Gemma 4 26B. The capacity is served by Eigen Labs' Darkbloom. You can try these models at no cost through OpenRouter's single API.

## Key points

- gpt-oss-20b (OpenAI's open-weight model) is available free on OpenRouter.
- Gemma 4 26B (Google) is available free on OpenRouter.
- The free capacity is provided by Eigen Labs' Darkbloom inference.
- OpenRouter gives one OpenAI-compatible API for many models, so you can swap models easily.
