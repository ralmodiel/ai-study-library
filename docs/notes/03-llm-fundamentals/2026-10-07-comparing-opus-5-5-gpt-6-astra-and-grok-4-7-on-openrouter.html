Summary

A model comparison of Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra and Grok 4.7 using OpenRouter, which gives access to many models through one platform. This is useful when choosing a model.

Key points

  • OpenRouter lets you compare frontier models side by side.
  • The models compared are Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Astra and Grok 4.7.
