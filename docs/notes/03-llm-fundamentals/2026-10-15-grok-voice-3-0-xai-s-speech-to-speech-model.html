Summary

Grok Voice 3.0 is presented as xAI's most intelligent speech-to-speech model. The announcement says it is aimed at resolving customer calls, with responses in under a second and costs below other frontier models.

Key points

  • It is a speech-to-speech model: audio in, audio out.
  • It is aimed at customer-call resolution.
  • Claimed response time is under one second.
  • It is claimed to cost less than other frontier voice models.
