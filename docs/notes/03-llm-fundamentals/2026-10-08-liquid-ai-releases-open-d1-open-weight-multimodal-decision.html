Liquid AI releases Open d1 open-weight multimodal decision models

Summary

Melvin Vivas shares Liquid AI's release of Open d1, two open-weight multimodal decision models. d1-3B handles text and vision; d1-omni-600M handles text with image or audio. Liquid AI targets real-time decision making from data centers down to edge devices.

Key points

  • Open d1 includes two open-weight models in Liquid AI's d1 decision model family.
  • d1-3B: text + vision.
  • d1-omni-600M: text + image, or text + audio.
  • Built for real-time decisions on NVIDIA hardware, from DGX in data centers to RTX workstations to Jetson at the edge.
