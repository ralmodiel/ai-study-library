Summary

The creator points to demos from Fastino Labs built on their GLiNER-based decision models. Fastino says decision models have pushed people to try use cases beyond parsing text, and their team built several demos to show this.

Key points

  • GLiNER models are being extended from entity extraction to 'decision' tasks.
  • Decision models encourage use cases beyond plain text parsing.
  • Fastino Labs published several demos of decision models.
  • Lots of community decision-model demos have circulated in recent weeks.
