# Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite Released for Agents

## Summary

Google released new Gemini models aimed at faster, cheaper agents. According to the announcement, Gemini 3.6 Flash uses fewer tokens than 3.5 Flash and delivers higher-quality work at the same cost. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is a fast, low-cost option for everyday tasks.

## Key points

- Gemini 3.6 Flash uses fewer tokens than 3.5 Flash, with higher quality at the same price.
- Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is a fast, cost-effective model for everyday tasks.
- The release is aimed at making AI agents faster, smarter and cheaper at scale.
