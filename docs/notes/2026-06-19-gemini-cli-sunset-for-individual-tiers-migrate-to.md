# Gemini CLI sunset for individual tiers: migrate to Antigravity CLI

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-06-19 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2067700189163651507)

**Topics:** AI Dev Tools & Productivity, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** beginner

## Summary

The post shares news that Gemini CLI stopped serving requests for Google AI Pro, Google AI Ultra and free-tier individual accounts. Users on those tiers have to move to Antigravity CLI. It shows how quickly AI coding tools can change.

## Key points

- Gemini CLI stopped serving requests for Google AI Pro, Google AI Ultra and free-tier individual accounts (June 2026).
- Users on those tiers must migrate to Antigravity CLI.
- Lesson: don't tie your workflow too tightly to one vendor's coding CLI, because tools can be retired quickly.
