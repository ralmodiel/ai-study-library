## Summary

Melvin Vivas congratulates Cognition, the maker of the AI software-engineering agent Devin, on passing $1B in annualized revenue run rate. The attached video is a marketing reel of customer testimonials. Its main claim is that every company is now a software company, and that autonomous coding agents covering the whole software development lifecycle can roughly double engineering output. There's no technical lesson here. It's useful only as a signal of where the market is heading.

## Key points

- Cognition (maker of Devin) says it has passed $1B in annualized revenue run rate, which shows real enterprise spending on AI coding agents.
- Framing used in the video: in 2026 every company is a software company. Goldman Sachs, Mercedes and the US government are given as examples.
- Customers say they saw almost 2x output over three months and doubled their engineering throughput with Devin. These are customer claims from a promotional video, not independent benchmarks.
- Devin is pitched as working across the whole software development lifecycle, not just autocompleting code.
- Customers describe the vendor relationship as a 'partnership' where their feature requests get heard. That points to how much enterprise adoption depends on vendor support.
- What it means for your career: knowing how to work with and supervise autonomous coding agents is becoming a mainstream engineering skill.
