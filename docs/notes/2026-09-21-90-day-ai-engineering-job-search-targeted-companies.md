## Summary

Bashiri Smith lays out a 90-day plan for landing an AI engineering job using only a laptop. Use Claude to make a list of 50 companies where your background is an asset. Find matching roles and connect with people from the hiring department on LinkedIn. Then turn the job description into a project idea, pitch it in a short Loom video, and send that video to your new connections so you stand out and could get a referral.

## Key points

- Prompt Claude for 50 companies where your background, past experience or accomplishments make you valuable, with a reason for each fit.
- Ask Claude where each company posts its jobs, then search the company's careers page for your target role.
- Note which department the role is in. On LinkedIn, open the company's People tab, search for that department name and connect with as many people as you can.
- Paste the full job description into a prompt to get a detailed project idea that this hiring team would care about. Some listings even describe example team projects.
- Record a Loom video covering your project idea, the problem it solves, and how the job description inspired it.
- Send the Loom to everyone who accepted your connection request. It gives them something specific to reply to.
- Opening with "Here's how I'd approach a problem your team is working on" works better than "Are you hiring?" and can move your application up or earn you a referral.
