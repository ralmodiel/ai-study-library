A100 80GB GPUs in Google Colab via Google AI Pro plans

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 10 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Follow Melvin Vivas: Melvin Vivas on X (@melvindvivas) · Melvin Vivas on X (@donvito) · Melvin Vivas on GitHub · Melvin Vivas's website (donvitocodes.com) · Melvin Vivas's blog (melvinvivas.com)

Summary

Google AI Pro subscriptions now bundle premium Google Colab benefits, including A100 GPUs with 80GB VRAM, and Ultra subscribers get H100s. That headroom lets you run and fine-tune mid-sized open models like Gemma 4 right in the browser.

Key points

  • Google AI Pro plans include premium Colab benefits with A100 GPUs (80GB VRAM).
  • Google AI Ultra subscribers can also use H100 GPUs in Colab.
  • 80GB VRAM is enough to run Gemma 4 31B in bf16 for inference.
  • You can do a full fine-tune of models up to Gemma 4 E4B in bf16.
  • LoRA tuning works on even larger models (the quoted post is cut off before giving the size).
  • No local GPU setup is needed. It all runs in the browser through Colab notebooks.
