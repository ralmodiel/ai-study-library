## Summary

Tip: when you train a model, ask Codex to help prepare the dataset. It can research the data format your base model needs.

## Key points

- Ask a coding agent to prepare your training dataset.
- Codex can look up the chat or template format that your chosen base model requires.
- This saves you from checking format details by hand before fine-tuning.
