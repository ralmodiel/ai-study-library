Train Your Own Decision Model Locally with Unsloth

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 8 October 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-10-08 · Open on X

Topics: Fine-tuning & Model Customization · Level: intermediate

Summary

Melvin shares news that you can now train your own 'decision model' on a base LLM such as Qwen3.5 or Gemma 4 using Unsloth's open-source repo. The quoted post says fine-tuning Qwen3.5 0.8B raised its aggregate accuracy from 20.7% to 74.3% across 3 decision benchmarks, using only 4GB of VRAM.

Key points

  • Decision models can be fine-tuned from small open base models like Qwen3.5 or Gemma 4.
  • The Qwen3.5 0.8B result: aggregate accuracy rose from 20.7% to 74.3% across 3 decision benchmarks.
  • Training ran locally on just 4GB of VRAM.
  • The tooling is Unsloth's open-source repo.
