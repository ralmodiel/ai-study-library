Fine-tuned Qwen3.5-9B beat frontier models for Coinbase fraud task

Summary

A quoted post says Coinbase fine-tuned the open-weight Qwen3.5-9B on its own internal data for fraud prevention. The fine-tuned model beat frontier models on that task and was cheaper and faster. The author expects more companies to fine-tune small open models for specialized tasks.

Key points

  • Coinbase fine-tuned the open-weight Qwen3.5-9B on proprietary internal data
  • Task: fraud prevention (a narrow, specialized task)
  • The fine-tuned 9B model beat frontier models on this task
  • It was much cheaper and faster than the frontier models
  • Trend: fine-tuning small open models on your own data for specialized enterprise tasks
