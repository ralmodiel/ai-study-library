# Small Local Models + Fine-Tuning Instead of More Compute (Liquid AI, LEAP)

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-08-09 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2086156825297318213)

**Topics:** Fine-tuning & Model Customization, LLM Fundamentals, Industry Trends & Job Market · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

Melvin argues that instead of chasing more compute, you should make small local models work for your specific use case. He points to Liquid AI's small models, which can even run on phones, and to LEAP, Liquid AI's framework for fine-tuning a model for your use case.

## Key points

- Don't chase more compute. Fit small local models to your specific use case instead.
- Liquid AI makes small models that are a good starting point.
- Liquid AI models are small enough to run on phones.
- LEAP is Liquid AI's fine-tuning framework for training a model for your use case.
