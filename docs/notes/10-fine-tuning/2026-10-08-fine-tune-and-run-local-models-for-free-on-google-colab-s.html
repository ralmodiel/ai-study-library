Fine-tune and Run Local Models for Free on Google Colab's T4 GPU

Summary

Melvin Vivas shares a GitHub collection of notebooks for fine-tuning local (open-weight) models and running inference on them. All of the notebooks run on the free T4 GPU in Google Colab, so you don't have to buy a GPU. The video explains how to get started: open Colab, sign in with a Google account, and run any notebook on the free GPU, which has about 15 GB of VRAM.

Key points

  • You don't need to buy a GPU to experiment with fine-tuning and inference on local models.
  • Go to colab.research.google.com (Google Colab) and sign in with your Gmail/Google account.
  • Colab's free tier gives you an NVIDIA T4 GPU with about 15 GB of VRAM.
  • You can run any notebook you find in Colab on that free GPU.
  • The creator's notebooks repo (github.com/donvito/...) covers both fine-tuning and inference, and every notebook is made to fit the free T4.

Try this

  • Open colab.research.google.com and sign in with your Google/Gmail account.
  • Set the Colab runtime to the free T4 GPU.
  • Find the creator's notebooks repo on GitHub (donvito). The caption link is cut off. Then open a fine-tuning or inference notebook in Colab and run it.
  • Fine-tune a small local model with one of the notebooks on Colab's free T4, then run inference with the result.
