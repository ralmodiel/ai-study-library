# Idea: Fine-Tune a Small Model on Your Best Tweets

Melvin Vivas · X post · 2026-09-13 · [Open on X](https://x.com/melvindvivas/status/2099004662691365226)

**Topics:** Fine-tuning & Model Customization, Portfolio Projects · **Level:** intermediate

## Summary

The creator plans to fine-tune a small model to write in his own style, using his most successful posts as the training dataset. If it works, he will open-source the model so others can use it with Bionic.

## Key points

- Use your own high-performing posts as a curated fine-tuning dataset.
- Fine-tune a small model to copy one person's writing style.
- Small fine-tuned models can be open-sourced and plugged into other tools.
