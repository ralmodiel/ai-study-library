Looking for a Low-Cost Replacement for Hugging Face AutoTrain

This note summarises a post by Melvin Vivas on X, posted 27 March 2026. The original belongs to Melvin Vivas.

Summary

The creator notes that Hugging Face AutoTrain has been discontinued and asks for a cheaper alternative, since Google AutoML and Amazon SageMaker cost too much for him. It's useful mainly as a list of managed or no-code fine-tuning platforms.

Key points

  • Hugging Face AutoTrain is reported as discontinued.
  • Google AutoML and Amazon SageMaker are managed training options, but the creator says they cost too much for him.
  • No alternative is given in the post.

Resources mentioned
