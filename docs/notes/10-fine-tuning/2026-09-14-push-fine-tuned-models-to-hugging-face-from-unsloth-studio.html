Push Fine-Tuned Models to Hugging Face from Unsloth Studio

Topics: Fine-tuning & Model Customization, LLMOps, Deployment & Monitoring · Level: intermediate

Summary

After post-training, Unsloth Studio can upload your model to Hugging Face from its UI. You can also choose the quantizations.

Key points

  • Unsloth Studio uploads post-trained models to the Hugging Face Hub from its UI.
  • You can configure the quantizations (for example GGUF variants) before you upload.
