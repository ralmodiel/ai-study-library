Liquid AI Cookbook: Fine-Tuning LFMs with CPT, SFT, DPO and GRPO

Summary

Melvin Vivas recommends the Liquid AI cookbook as a strong, underrated resource for fine-tuning Liquid's LFM models. It covers text, vision, audio and encoder models. It also covers several training methods (CPT, SFT, DPO, GRPO) using Unsloth or Hugging Face TRL.

Key points

  • The Liquid AI cookbook covers fine-tuning text, vision, audio and encoder models.
  • Training methods covered: continued pretraining (CPT), supervised fine-tuning (SFT), DPO and GRPO.
  • Examples use Unsloth or Hugging Face TRL to fine-tune LFM (Liquid Foundation Model) models.

Try this
