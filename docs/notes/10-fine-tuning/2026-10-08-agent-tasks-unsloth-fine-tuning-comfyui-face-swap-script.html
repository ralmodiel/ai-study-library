Agent tasks: Unsloth fine-tuning, ComfyUI face swap, script writing

Topics: Fine-tuning & Model Customization, AI Agents, Tool Use & MCP, AI Dev Tools & Productivity · Level: intermediate

Summary

The creator lists tasks his Grok Bots are handling. These include building Colab notebooks to fine-tune small open models (Qwen3.5-4B, Gemma4-E4B) into decision models with Unsloth, face swapping in video with ComfyUI and Minimax H3, and drafting YouTube Shorts scripts. It shows how to hand concrete AI-engineering tasks to agents.

Key points

  • Agents can be given whole engineering tasks, such as writing a Colab notebook for fine-tuning.
  • Small models around 4B parameters (Qwen3.5-4B, Gemma4-E4B) can be fine-tuned into task-specific 'decision models'.
  • Unsloth is the library named for efficient fine-tuning, and it runs in Google Colab.
  • ComfyUI plus the Minimax H3 model are used for face swapping in video.
  • Agents can also handle content pre-production, such as YouTube Shorts scripts.
