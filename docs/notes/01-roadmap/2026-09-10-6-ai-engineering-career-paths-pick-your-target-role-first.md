## Summary

This short reel lists six different AI engineering roles and gives a one-line description of each. Its main point is that "getting into AI" is too vague a goal. Each role needs a different set of skills, so choosing a specific role early helps you focus your learning and reach it faster.

## Key points

- Machine Learning Engineer: trains and optimizes ML models.
- Applied AI Engineer: builds AI-powered products with LLMs and modern software engineering.
- AI Agent Engineer: builds autonomous agents that reason, plan and use tools.
- MLOps Engineer: deploys, monitors and scales AI systems in production.
- AI Research Engineer: improves models through experiments and cutting-edge research.
- AI Solutions Engineer: designs AI solutions that solve real business problems.
- Each path needs different skills. The sooner you pick your target role, the sooner you become an AI engineer.
- The creator claims these roles can pay $100K–$200K+.
