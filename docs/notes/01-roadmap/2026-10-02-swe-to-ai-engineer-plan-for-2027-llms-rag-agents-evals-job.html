SWE-to-AI Engineer Plan for 2027: LLMs, RAG, Agents, Evals, Job Search

Summary

A short reel whose audio is only music. All the lessons are in the caption: a 7-step plan for software engineers moving into AI engineering going into 2027. You learn how LLMs work, build a RAG app and an agent, evaluate both, ship them as open source to real users, and turn the results into resume bullets and interview answers. It ends with job-search advice: search by skill keywords, not just job titles.

Key points

  • Learn how LLMs work: tokens, embeddings, context windows, and when to use different models.
  • Build a RAG app with hybrid search, reranking, and citations, and work out why it sometimes retrieves the wrong information.
  • Build an agent that researches companies or updates a CRM, with retries and a human approval step.
  • Evaluation is what separates a wannabe from a $200k AI engineer. Measure retrieval quality, answer accuracy, task success, latency, and cost for both projects.
  • Open-source your projects and get real users. Fix what breaks and measure what improves.
  • Write resume bullets that show the problem you solved, the tools you used, and the impact.
  • Practice explaining your decisions in interviews: Why this model? Why RAG? What happens when a tool fails?
  • Search by skill, not just title. The creator says over 50% of companies label AI job openings poorly. Try AI Engineer, Forward-Deployed Engineer, and AI Solutions Engineer, and also search keywords like RAG, RAGAS, Agents, LLM, and LangChain.
