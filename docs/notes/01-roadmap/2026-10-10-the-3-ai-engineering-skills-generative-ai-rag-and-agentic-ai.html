The 3 AI Engineering Skills: Generative AI, RAG, and Agentic AI

This note summarises a reel by Bashiri Smith on Facebook, posted 10 October 2026. The original belongs to Bashiri Smith.

Summary

A quick roadmap that names three skill areas as the core of today's AI engineering job market: generative AI, RAG and agentic AI. It suggests 1–2 hours of study a day for six weeks, working through LLM basics first, then retrieval systems, then agents built with frameworks like CrewAI, LangGraph, LlamaIndex and the OpenAI Agents SDK. A free roadmap PDF is linked in the caption.

Key points

  • Generative AI, RAG and agents are the three skills the creator says drive AI engineering hiring right now (he claims they can lead to $200k+ roles).
  • Suggested pace: 1–2 hours a day for about 6 weeks.
  • Step 1, generative AI: how LLMs work, prompting, model APIs, transformers, context windows and embeddings.
  • Step 2, RAG: vector databases, chunking, retrieval, re-ranking, semantic search, and how companies connect LLMs to their own data.
  • Step 3, agentic AI: tool use, workflows, MCP, memory, reasoning loops, orchestration frameworks and multi-agent systems.
  • Frameworks to learn for agents: CrewAI, LangGraph, LlamaIndex and the OpenAI Agents SDK.
  • The YouTube videos and courses shown on screen are not named in the transcript; the caption PDF is the full resource list.

Try this

  • Study for 1–2 hours a day for about six weeks.
  • Start with generative AI basics: how LLMs work, prompting, APIs, transformers, context windows and embeddings.
  • Then learn RAG: vector databases, chunking, retrieval, re-ranking and semantic search.
  • Then learn agentic AI: tool use, MCP, memory, reasoning loops and multi-agent systems.
  • Get hands-on with CrewAI, LangGraph, LlamaIndex and the OpenAI Agents SDK.
