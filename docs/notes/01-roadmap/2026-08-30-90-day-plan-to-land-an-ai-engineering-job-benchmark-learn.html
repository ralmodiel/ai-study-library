90-Day Plan to Land an AI Engineering Job: Benchmark, Learn, Build

Summary

The creator lays out a 90-day plan for getting an AI engineering job. First, build a small coding project without AI help to find your skill gaps. Then study those gaps every day while taking a DeepLearning.AI course and working with models on Hugging Face. Finally, research the companies you want to work for and build an MVP that solves a real problem one of their teams has, so recruiters notice you.

Key points

  • Start with a benchmark coding project. It takes about 1-5 hours, you must build it without AI help, and it does not go on your resume. Its only job is to show your skill gaps.
  • Spend 2-4 hours a day fixing the skills the benchmark showed you lack.
  • At the same time, take a DeepLearning.AI course (the video doesn't name it) and do at least one lesson every day.
  • Keep your hands-on skills sharp by working with models on Hugging Face.
  • To build a portfolio project recruiters notice, list 10-50 target companies and look at their job openings.
  • Find the most common opening, look up the team posting it, work out what problems that team has, and build an MVP around one of them.
  • The creator says the full 15-minute project training and all resource links come free if you comment '15'.
