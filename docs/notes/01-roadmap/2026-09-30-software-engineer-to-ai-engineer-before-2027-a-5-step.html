Summary

Bashiri Smith lays out a plan for software engineers to move into AI engineering. Step one is learning five skill categories from a structured roadmap. The first three matter most and he says they can take about three months. After that you build portfolio projects, rewrite your resume around AI work, and start applying right away, even before you feel ready. He ends by pointing to his BASWE.Ai Engineer community, which runs daily live calls with engineers and recruiters.

Key points

  • Your existing software engineering fundamentals are an advantage. You only need to add five AI engineering skill categories.
  • The first three categories are the most important. If you're dedicated, you can learn them in about three months by following a roadmap of courses in order.
  • Build real AI engineering projects that go beyond basic tutorials. He has a video with 27 project examples.
  • Rework your resume: put AI technologies you've actually used in the skills section, add AI bullet points with measurable results, and replace old projects with your new AI projects.
  • Job titles: in the video he says to relabel roles since 2023 as 'AI Engineer', but the written caption says to keep titles and experience accurate. Follow the caption.
  • Start applying immediately, even if you don't feel ready. Keep learning, building, and practicing interviews while you apply.
  • He says he's never seen anyone who follows the application steps for 2–4 hours a day fail to land a role.
  • Get feedback from people who do this work, such as engineers and recruiters, on projects, interviews, your resume, and LinkedIn.

Try this

  • Follow the structured AI engineering roadmap and focus first on the three most important skill categories. Aim to finish them in about three months.
  • Build AI engineering projects from the 27 project ideas.
  • Update your resume: add an AI engineer summary, list the AI technologies you've used, write AI bullet points with measurable results, and swap old projects for AI projects. Keep titles accurate.
  • Start applying immediately, even if you don't feel ready, and spend 2–4 hours a day on the application steps.
  • Keep learning, building, and practicing interviews while you apply.
  • Get feedback on your projects, resume, LinkedIn, and interviews from working AI engineers and recruiters.
