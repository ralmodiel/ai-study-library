## Summary

Claude Opus 5.5 is now available in Cursor. It is the new top model on CursorBench at 57.8% (Max mode) and costs 40% less per task than Opus 5. It's a useful data point for choosing a model inside your coding assistant.

## Key points

- Claude Opus 5.5 is available in Cursor.
- It is the new #1 model on CursorBench with 57.8% (Max mode).

- Cost per task, not just accuracy, is a key metric when choosing a coding model.
