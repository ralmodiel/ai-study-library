## Summary

The creator says he uses Cursor Automations to keep the AI news page on his website updated with fresh news every day. It is an example of using a coding assistant's automation feature to maintain content on a schedule.

- The result is a daily-refreshed AI news page at donvitocodes.com/ai-news.
- Scheduled agent automations can keep content up to date without manual edits.
