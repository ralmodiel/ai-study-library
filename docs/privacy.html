Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-02

This page explains what happens to your data when you use the AI Engineer Study Library at ralmodiel.github.io/ai-study-library. The site is run by Roque Almodiel, a private individual, as a free study resource. It has no accounts, no ads and no sign-up, and it sells nothing.

Who to contact

Roque Almodiel is responsible for this site. Contact them by opening an issue at github.com/ralmodiel/ai-study-library/issues (issues are public, so do not include personal details there; ask for a private channel instead).

What stays on your device

Your ticked checkboxes, the tab you were on, and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and you can clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you load the page and logs it for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The fonts are served from this site too, so loading the page does not contact Google.

Google Analytics (only if you accept)

When you first visit, a banner asks whether this site may use Google Analytics. Until you click Accept, Google's script is not loaded and nothing is sent to Google. If you accept, Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) receives:

  • the pages and tabs you open and how long you stay,
  • what you type in the search box (searches that look like an email address or phone number are never sent),
  • the site that referred you, your browser, device type, screen size and language,
  • your approximate location (country and city), which Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_CSFCML3KMM, which keep a random ID so repeat visits can be counted. They expire after at most 13 months. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off: the data is not used for advertising and is not linked to your Google account.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the cookie consent rules of the ePrivacy Directive). Purpose: to see how many people use the library and which parts are useful. Retention: event data is kept in Google Analytics for 2 months, then deleted.

Changing your mind: use Cookie settings (here or at the bottom of the library) at any time. Declining after accepting stops measurement and deletes the cookies.

Google may process this data in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the Data Privacy Act in the Philippines) you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, object to its use, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Because the analytics data has no name or account attached, it usually cannot be matched to you, but you can always decline or withdraw consent so none is collected. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Links to other sites

The library links to many other sites (Facebook, X, YouTube, course providers and more). Their own privacy policies apply once you follow a link.
