6-month plan: from the ground up

Twenty-four weeks for people starting with little programming: a month of software basics (Python, Git, SQL and APIs), then the 3-month plan's capstone, an assistant over documents you know, with extra weeks for a web front end, data pipelines, production RAG, agent frameworks, Docker, the cloud, Kubernetes, system design and forward-deployed work. About 8 hours a week. The topics follow the learning path; other plans: 1-month plan · 2-month plan · 3-month plan.

An unofficial study plan built from the public posts of Bashiri Smith and Melvin Vivas, not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Items under Fill the gap are not from the creators: they cover skills their posts don't. Track your progress in the library app; ticks are saved in your browser.
